Internet & WiFi

Slow internet, router setup, WiFi dead zones — fixes that actually work.

Slow internet has only a few real causes, but they hide well: the router's placement, the cable, the provider's line, or the device itself. The guides in this section separate those quickly — test at the router, test on another device, test at a different hour — before recommending a single purchase. Coverage includes WiFi that dies in far rooms, connections that drop every few minutes, red router lights, the connected-but-no-internet trap, and why nights run slower than mornings. DNS changes, channel fixes and mesh advice all come with honest limits of what each can and cannot fix.
