Smart TV & Streaming

Smart TV WiFi, apps and streaming issues — Netflix, YouTube and everything in between.

Smart TVs fail in uniquely annoying ways: apps that won't install, WiFi that connects to nothing, screens that go black while sound keeps playing. This section covers the lot — Netflix errors, remote problems, vertical lines, random shutdowns and casting failures. TV menus differ by brand, so the guides name where Samsung, LG and Android TV hide the relevant settings. The full power-drain restart (unplug for a whole minute) fixes more than it should; the guides explain why, and what to try when it doesn't.
