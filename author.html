Hi, I'm Abdul Rafay — founder and author of VelNexis.

My Background

I'm currently completing my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science. Studying CS means constantly working with how software, devices, networks and the web actually function — and that knowledge goes straight into the guides on this site.

My Experience

Over the past few years I've built and worked on several tech websites and blogs. That journey taught me what readers actually need: clear steps, honest verdicts, and answers that respect their time.

Why I Started VelNexis

The tech internet is full of noise — viral rumors, confusing fixes and reviews that read like ads. VelNexis is my answer to that: one place where Windows, Android and iPhone fixes are written step by step, verdicts are honest, and everything is explained in plain English.

What I Do Here

Researching, writing, editing, and maintaining the site — it's all me. If you ever spot an error, tell me and it gets fixed.

How I Verify What I Publish

Every guide has to survive three questions before it ships: is this how the problem actually behaves in the field, does the official documentation agree, and could a reader follow it at 2am with a dead printer and no patience? If a step fails any of those, it doesn't go live. For rumors the bar is higher still: no verdict without evidence you can check yourself.

Beyond VelNexis

When I'm not writing or studying, I'm usually following AI developments, trying out new apps and tools, and keeping an eye on where tech headlines actually come from. My code and projects live on GitHub: github.com/wincobvius.
