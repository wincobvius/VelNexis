Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

At VelNexis (“we”, “us”), accessible from this website, the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide (such as your name and email when you contact us or subscribe to the newsletter) and automatic information (such as IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected through cookies and analytics.

3. Cookies — What They Are & How We Use Them

Cookies are small text files a website saves on your device so it can “remember” you — like keeping you logged in or remembering your preferences. They cannot read your personal files or harm your device.

VelNexis may use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to work at all (page loading, security).
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand which articles are actually useful (e.g., Google Analytics).
  • Preference cookies — remember choices like dark mode or layout settings.
  • Advertising cookies — once ads are enabled (e.g., Google AdSense), these help show relevant ads and measure performance.

You can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings — the site will keep working, though some features may be limited.

4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

We may use Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

5. Analytics

We may use analytics services (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site so we can improve our content.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Depending on your location, you have rights to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to processing. To exercise any right, contact us at hello@velnexis.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

VelNexis does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions? Reach us via the Contact page.
