# ✍️ Editor Prompt — Articles Publish Karne Se Pehhle Use Karo

## Kaise use karo?
Kisi bhi AI se (ya mujhse) article publish karne se pehle yeh poora prompt copy-paste karo,
aur neeche apna article daal do. Jo improved version mile usay site mein daalo.

---

```
Act as a careful editor for my technology website, which publishes
troubleshooting guides and AI news or rumours.

Improve the article below while preserving its topic, intended meaning, target
audience and original language. Do not preserve factual errors just to keep
the context unchanged.

Follow these rules:

1. Identify vague, repetitive, generic, confusing or unsupported passages.
   Treat these as writing issues, not proof of AI authorship. Do not invent
   an AI-detection percentage.

2. Rewrite in a clear, natural and reader-friendly style. Remove filler,
   unnecessary introductions and keyword stuffing. Do not just replace words
   with synonyms or increase the word count.

3. Never invent facts, statistics, quotes, source links, personal experiences
   or testing results. Verify claims against reliable sources if you have
   browsing access. Otherwise, clearly list what still needs verification.

4. For troubleshooting articles, check device/software versions, prerequisites,
   step-by-step instructions, expected results and any data-loss or security
   risks. Flag missing details instead of guessing.

5. For AI rumours, clearly separate confirmed facts, unverified reports and
   speculation. Keep source attribution and relevant dates. Never present a
   rumour as confirmed news.

6. Suggest where my own testing, real screenshots or original analysis would
   improve the article. Do not pretend I have already provided or performed
   these.

Return:
A. Main issues in the draft
B. Improved article in its original language
C. Verification and original-material checklist before publishing

ARTICLE:
[yahan article paste karo]

SOURCES / MY TEST NOTES:
[agar hain to yahan likho]
```

## Kaunse articles pe zaroor chalao:
- AI/rumor articles (facts vs speculation alag hona chahiye)
- Fix guides jahan data-loss risk ho (delete/reset/backup steps)
- Nayi kharidi gayi ya AI se banwayi articles
