WhatsApp & Social

WhatsApp hacks, message issues, and social media account issues — fast, practical help.

WhatsApp is where daily life happens, so a ban, a stuck backup or a hacked account is a genuinely bad day. This section covers the real cases: accounts banned by mistake and how appeals actually work, backups stuck halfway, one tick that never becomes two, chats lost to a reset, and what Meta AI does and does not see. The guides stay inside official tools — linked devices, two-step verification, chat backup — because third-party recovery apps are how accounts get stolen in the first place. Every guide includes the prevention step that stops the problem returning.

Two safeguards prevent nearly everything here: two-step verification on, and chat backup scheduled. The recovery guides — bans, hacks, stuck backups — move fast because speed matters; a hijacked account is easiest to reclaim in the first hour. One tick mysteries are usually offline phones, not blocks. And if a guide mentions a third-party recovery app, it is warning you away from it. Keep your backup healthy even when nothing is wrong: run a manual backup once a month and check it completes.
