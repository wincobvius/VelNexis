Android Issues

This section covers the problems Android users actually search for: battery drain that starts overnight, Play Store downloads that refuse to move, notifications that go silent, storage that vanishes into chat media. The guides are step by step and name the exact settings menus, so you are not guessing which screen to open. Where a fix depends on the brand — Samsung, Xiaomi, Infinix — the differences are called out. Nothing here needs root access or paid apps.
