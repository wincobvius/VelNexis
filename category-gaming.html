Gaming

Lag fixes, performance guides, settings and honest hardware takes.

Gaming problems split into two camps: performance (lag, stutter, low FPS) and hardware (consoles that overheat, drives that won't read discs, controllers that disconnect). This section handles both. On PC, the guides show how to find the real bottleneck — CPU, GPU, RAM or storage — before changing a single setting, because guessing wastes money. On console, they cover the loud PS4, the blinking blue light, PS5 disc issues and Switch charging faults, with the safe-mode steps that fix most software causes. Every guide tries the fixes that cost nothing first.

Performance work has one rule: measure before changing. Task Manager while playing tells you more than any forum post, and the guides here are built around that check. Console owners, the safe-mode and power-drain steps fix more than their simplicity suggests — run them before assuming hardware death. Thermal paste and dust cleanings are the two maintenance jobs that keep every guide in this section from being needed twice. Console or PC, the order stays the same: free fixes, then maintenance, then parts. The monitor-cable guide deserves a read even if your display works — half of all high-refresh setups run at 60Hz because of one cheap cable, and nobody notices for years.
