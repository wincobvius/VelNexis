Get in Touch

Questions, tips, rumor reports, or corrections — we usually reply within 48 hours.

Before you write — it makes replies faster

A good message gets a good answer, usually the same day. A vague one goes back and forth for a week. If you are reporting a tech problem, include four things: the device (phone model or PC specs), the operating system and its version, the exact error message if there is one, and what you already tried. With those four lines, most problems get a real diagnosis in the first reply instead of questions.

Reporting a viral rumor? Paste the message or screenshot text you received, and mention where it reached you — a WhatsApp group, a YouTube video, a forwarded SMS. Origin matters: half of every rumor check is tracing where the claim started, and your first-hand source is often the missing piece.

Found an error in a guide? Say which article and which step, and what happened when you followed it. Corrections get priority treatment — wrong steps on a site people use for fixes are the one thing we take more seriously than rumors.

One honest note: there is no phone line and no live chat. This is a one-author publication, and written replies are how it stays accurate — every answer can be checked, linked and corrected later.
