Every guide in this section assumes work, not luck. The realistic path — build a skill, land the first clients, protect yourself from non-payers — is shorter than people fear and longer than courses promise. The scam guides exist because the same tricks recycle every year with new names. If a method cannot survive the question how exactly does this make money, walk away. Whatever path you pick from this section, write down your first realistic milestone before starting — first order, first client, first payout — and treat anything promising it in days, not months, as a warning sign rather than a shortcut.
