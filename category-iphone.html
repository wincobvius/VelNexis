iPhone & Apple

iPhone, iPad and Apple device issues — fixes explained simply, no genius bar needed.

iPhones hide their problems behind polish — until Face ID stops recognizing you, the battery dies by evening, or the phone sticks on the Apple logo. This section covers the iPhone issues that come up most: charging problems, forgotten passcodes, iCloud storage walls, water damage and the slowdown that follows some iOS updates. The guides use Apple's own recovery tools first, because they are free and safe, and they say plainly when a problem is hardware and needs service. If a guide involves erasing data, that warning comes first.
