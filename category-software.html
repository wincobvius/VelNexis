Software & Apps

App and software issues — installing, updating, crashing and 'it stopped working' fixes.

Software problems are rarely about the software itself — they are about installers, licenses, permissions and leftovers from old versions. This section handles the grind: programs that refuse to install or uninstall, product keys that vanished, Office asking for activation again and again, and version rollbacks when an update makes things worse. Each guide explains what the error actually means, then gives the fix in order of what works most often. Free and official tools are preferred, and the traps — activators, random old-version download sites — are named so you can avoid them.
