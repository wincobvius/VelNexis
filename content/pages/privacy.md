At VelNexis (“we”, “us”), accessible from this website, the privacy of our visitors is one of our main priorities. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 2. Information We Collect

## 3. Cookies — What They Are & How We Use Them

Cookies are small text files a website saves on your device so it can “remember” you — like keeping you logged in or remembering your preferences. They cannot read your personal files or harm your device.

VelNexis may use the following types of cookies:

- **Essential cookies** — required for the site to work at all (page loading, security).
- **Analytics cookies** — help us understand which articles are actually useful (e.g., Google Analytics).
- **Preference cookies** — remember choices like dark mode or layout settings.
- **Advertising cookies** — once ads are enabled (e.g., Google AdSense), these help show relevant ads and measure performance.

You can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings — the site will keep working, though some features may be limited.

## 4. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting **Google Ads Settings**.
