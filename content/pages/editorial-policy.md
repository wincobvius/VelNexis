%% How VelNexis researches, writes, reviews and corrects its guides.

## Who writes here

Every guide on VelNexis is planned, edited and published by Abdul Rafay, the site's founder and author — a computer science student who has spent years building and working on tech websites. Articles are drafted with modern writing tools in the workflow, and nothing goes live until it has been read, checked and approved by him. Where a topic needs knowledge the site cannot stand behind, the article says so instead of guessing.

## How a guide gets written

1. **Start from a real question.** Guides begin with the problems people actually search for — a printer printing blank pages, a phone dying by evening, a stuck withdrawal.
2. **Explain what is going wrong first.** A fix you understand is a fix you can reuse. Each article says why the problem happens before the steps begin.
3. **Steps in the order that works.** Fixes are ordered by what solves the case most often, with the exact menu names you will see on screen.
4. **Warnings before risk.** Formatting, resets, disk tools — anything that can cost data carries a warning placed before the step, never after.
5. **The honest limit.** When a problem needs a repair shop, a lab, or a part replacement, the guide says that plainly.

## Corrections and updates

Technology moves — menus change, apps update, policies shift. Guides carry their publish date so you can judge freshness yourself. When a reader reports an error, the claim is checked, and if it holds, the guide is fixed and the date refreshed. Corrections are made silently and quickly — the goal is accurate pages, not announcements.

## What we do not do

- No paid verdicts. Nothing on this site recommends a product because someone paid for it.
- No invented testing. Articles do not claim benchmarks that never happened.
- No scare tactics. Security coverage explains risks without inventing nightmares.
- No content written only to rank. If a topic is not worth a reader's time, it is not worth a page.

## Trust transparency

VelNexis is an independent, reader-first publication. It carries no sponsored posts. When advertising opens through Google AdSense, ads will be labeled as ads and will never influence editorial text. Questions about any article — including challenges to its accuracy — are welcome through the [contact page](contact.html).
