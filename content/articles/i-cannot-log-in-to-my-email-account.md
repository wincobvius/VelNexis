You enter your email and password and get "wrong password", "account not found", or a loading error. The causes range from simple to serious: a typing mistake with Caps Lock on, an old saved password, a browser problem, a suspended account, or a hack. Sorting simple causes first saves a lot of panic.

## The simple causes first

1. **Check the boring things:** Caps Lock off, the full address typed correctly (no typos, right domain — @gmail vs @hotmail), and a working internet connection. An embarrassing share of "hacks" end here.
2. **Old saved password?** If the browser autofills an old password, type it manually instead — then reset it properly: click **"Forgot password"** and follow the recovery flow to your recovery phone or email.
3. **Try another browser or an incognito window.** A corrupted cache or a misbehaving extension breaks logins in ways that look like account problems. If incognito works, clear the browser's cache for that site and disable extensions one by one.
4. **Using the mail app rather than the website?** Remove the account from the app and add it again — app tokens expire and fail in confusing ways the website does not.

## The serious variants

5. **"Suspicious activity" or a security check message?** Complete the verification Google or Microsoft shows — it is designed to prove ownership, and it works from a device you have used before.
6. **Password "wrong" but you are certain, plus recovery options changed?** Those two together suggest the account is compromised — go straight to the account recovery page (google.com/accounts/recovery or account.live.com/acsr) and our hacked-account guide. Change connected passwords once you are back in.
