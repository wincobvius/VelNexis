Outlook (or Hotmail, the same Microsoft family) refuses your login — wrong password errors, account locked messages, or endless loading. Microsoft accounts get locked temporarily when it spots unusual sign-ins, and its passwords are often different from what people remember. The fix path is similar to Gmail's: reset, verify, unlock.

## Work through this order

1. **Retype carefully on the actual website** (outlook.com or login.live.com), not through a bookmarked or emailed link: Caps Lock off, the full address right — @hotmail.com, @outlook.com and @live.com are all Microsoft but different exact addresses.
2. **Reset the password** with "Forgot password": Microsoft verifies you via recovery email, recovery phone, or the authenticator app. If none of those is current, the account recovery form (account.live.com/acsr) asks details only the real owner tends to know — account creation, recent contacts, old subjects.
3. **"Account temporarily locked" message?** That is a security hold, not a punishment — it clears on its own (usually within hours to a day). Do not keep hammering login attempts; each try extends suspicion.
4. **App-specific problem?** If the website works but the Outlook app refuses, remove the account from the app and re-add it — expired sync tokens cause "endless loading" that looks like an account problem.
5. **Still blocked after recovery?** Microsoft support's virtual agent can escalate genuine cases with an identity check — have your recovery info at hand when you contact it.

## The prevention mirror

Same lesson as every account: recovery email + recovery phone + authenticator app, all current, all set while you still have access.
