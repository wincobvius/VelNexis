Your phone loses battery power very quickly, even when you are not using it much. A normal healthy phone should last a full day on one charge. This happens because of a bright screen, apps running in the background, weak network signal, or an old battery. Phones older than 2–3 years often have weak batteries that cannot hold charge like new ones. If you ignore it, the phone can start shutting down suddenly even with charge left.

## How to fix it

1. Open Settings → Battery. See which app uses the most battery.
2. Lower the screen brightness. Set screen timeout to 30 seconds.
3. Turn off WiFi, Bluetooth and Location when you are not using them.
4. Delete apps you never use.
5. If the phone is old, the battery may be weak. Get it changed from a shop.

## Real fixes for drain
- Check Settings > Battery for the top consumers first - one misbehaving app is usually the whole story.
- Set heavy apps to 'Restricted' background (Android battery settings) - it stops overnight chewing.
- Dark mode on OLED screens saves real power - often 10 percent a day.
- Turn off always-on display and shorten screen timeout to 30 seconds.
- Weak mobile signal drains battery fast, because the phone shouts louder in poor coverage. Use WiFi calling at home.
- Charging to 100 percent every night ages the battery - many phones now offer an 80 percent charge limit option.
- Battery health below 80 percent after 2+ years: settings tweaks cannot beat chemistry. A battery replacement transforms the phone.
- Cold weather makes batteries drain suddenly - keep the phone warm in winter.

Screenshot your battery usage screen at night for three days. The app list rarely surprises - but the screen-on time does, and most 'battery problems' turn out to be screen time problems.

The battery usage screen tells the truth about the drain. Match what tops your list to the row below before deleting anything.

## What the battery screen really says

| Battery screen shows | Real cause | What actually helps |
| Screen at the top | Brightness and always-on display | Lower brightness, shorten the timeout |
| One app at 60 percent plus | Background activity or a bug | Update it, then restrict background use |
| Android System high | Pending updates or wakelocks | Restart weekly, install system updates |
| Camera or location high | An app keeping sensors alive | Check which app recently used location |
