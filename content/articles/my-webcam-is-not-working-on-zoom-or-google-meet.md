Your camera works fine everywhere except in Zoom, Meet or Teams — the meeting shows a black square where your face should be. Video call apps manage camera access through their own settings plus system permissions, and a camera can only serve one app at a time. The meeting app's device list is the place people forget to check.

## How to fix it

1. Check the webcam works in another app (like the Windows Camera app).
2. In the meeting app settings, select the correct camera from the video device list.
3. Check Windows privacy: Settings → Privacy → Camera → allow desktop apps.
4. Close other apps using the camera (only one app can use it at a time).
5. Update the webcam driver or reinstall the app.

## Give the app your camera
- In the browser, click the camera icon in the address bar during a call and set Camera to 'Allow'. One wrong click blocks every future call.
- In Zoom or Meet settings, check which camera is selected - after a Windows update it often switches to a virtual camera.
- Windows: Settings > Privacy and security > Camera - turn on camera access for the system and for desktop apps.
- Close other apps that use the camera (Skype, Teams, OBS). Windows gives the camera to one app at a time.
- Unplug and replug a USB webcam, and try a different USB port.
- Some cameras have their own 'Enable' toggle in the manufacturer's app - check that too.

## Quick recap
- Allow the camera in the browser's address bar.
- Select the right camera inside the app.
- Windows privacy settings must allow desktop apps.
- One app at a time - Windows gives the camera to a single program.
