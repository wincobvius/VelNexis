First, the myth: **“Close all your apps to save battery.”** False. Android freezes background apps; force-closing them makes the phone re-launch everything from scratch — using *more* battery. Stop closing apps.

 

## What actually works

- **Find the real culprit:** Settings → Battery → per-app usage. One app is usually 40%+ of drain
- **Drop screen brightness / shorten sleep time** — the screen is drain #1 for almost everyone
- **Turn off “always-on display”**
- **Restrict background data** for social apps
- **Uninstall unused apps** that wake the phone (wakelocks)
- **Dark mode** on OLED screens — real, measurable savings
- **Location → “Only while using”** for every app that doesn’t need always-on GPS
- **Check battery health** — below 80% capacity, no software trick helps

Q: Do battery saver apps help?
A: No — they’re usually ad-heavy wake-lock abusers themselves. The built-in Battery Saver is enough.

## Quick wins for battery life
- Screen brightness on auto and a 30-second timeout - the screen is always the top drain.
- Settings > Battery names the worst apps - restrict the top ones from running in the background.
- Turn off always-on display and unnecessary location permissions.
- Dark mode on OLED screens saves real power, often 10 percent a day.
- Weak signal drains fast - the phone shouts louder in poor coverage. Use WiFi calling at home.
- Let battery saver switch on automatically at 20 percent.
- Avoid full 0-to-100 cycles; short charges between 20 and 80 are gentler.
- Heat is the enemy: a phone on a car dashboard or under a pillow while charging ages fast.
- After 2-3 years with health under 80 percent, a battery replacement beats every setting.
