One or more lights on your router turn red (or keep blinking) and there is no internet in the house. The lights are the router's language — green/blue means healthy, red means it is not receiving the internet signal from your company's line. Common causes: a loose or cut cable, the company's line is down in your area, or the router crashed after load shedding. Knowing the light colors saves you a useless complaint call.

## How to fix it

1. Red light usually means no signal from your internet company.
2. Check all cables are tight and not broken.
3. Unplug the router for 2 minutes, plug it back. Wait 5 minutes for lights to settle.
4. After rain or load shedding, fiber lines can fail. Wait some time.
5. If the red light stays, call your internet company. The line may be cut.

## Read the router lights
- Red internet light means the router cannot reach your provider. Unplug it for a full minute first, then look again.
- Reseat the main cable - phone line, DSL or fiber - firmly at both ends.
- Log in to the router (usually 192.168.1.1): the status page often names the exact problem.
- If the light stays red after a restart and cable check, the fault is outside your home. Call the provider's helpline - line faults are their job and free to fix.
- A red WiFi light is different - that is the wireless radio. A factory reset (hold the reset pin 10 seconds) fixes software causes.
- After a storm, red lights are common - power spikes kill modems. A surge protector is cheaper than a new router.
- Before calling, note which lights are which color - the helpline asks exactly this.
