Word or Excel works fine for a few days, then suddenly demands activation again — you enter the key, it works, then asks again. This loop happens when Office is logged into the wrong account, when the installation is damaged, or when multiple Office versions are fighting each other. Office's built-in repair tool usually ends the loop.

## How to fix it

1. Open any Office app → File → Account → check if it says "Product Activated".
2. If it shows "Sign in", sign in with the Microsoft account you used to buy Office.
3. Repair Office: Control Panel → Programs → Microsoft Office → Change → Quick Repair.
4. Check you are not signed in with a different Microsoft account than the purchase.
5. If it still asks, uninstall Office completely and install it again from your account page.

## Stop the key prompts
- If you bought a one-time Office (2019, 2021), sign in with the Microsoft account used to buy it - Office finds the license itself, no key needed.
- Check account.microsoft.com/services - all your digital licenses are listed there.
- Close Office completely, then open any Office app with 'Run as administrator' once - it lets activation complete.
- In Word: File > Account > Sign out, restart, sign back in.
- Microsoft 365 needs an active subscription - an expired payment card causes constant prompts. Check billing.
- If Office came 'free' with the PC, it was a trial, and trials end. Use free Office on the web (office.com) or LibreOffice instead.
- Avoid 'activator' tools - they are the classic malware carrier.

Before hunting for the key, sign out of Office completely, restart, and sign back in with the account that bought it. Half of all repeated prompts are a broken sign-in, not a missing license.
