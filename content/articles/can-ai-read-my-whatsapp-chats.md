WhatsApp sits on your most private conversations — family, money, documents — so "can AI read my chats?" is a fair question before typing anything sensitive.

## The short answer

Your normal, person-to-person WhatsApp chats are end-to-end encrypted. WhatsApp cannot read their contents, and Meta says these chats are not used to train its AI. The encryption itself uses the Signal protocol, which is publicly documented and has been reviewed by outside cryptographers — this is a stronger basis than a company promise.

## Where AI does come in

Meta's assistant processes only the messages where you deliberately involve it — tagging @Meta AI or opening a direct chat with it. Those interactions follow different rules from your private chats; check WhatsApp's current privacy policy for today's exact wording, because these policies do change.

## What encryption does not protect

- Backups, depending on your backup settings
- Screenshots or files you forward anywhere else
- Anything typed into other apps and services
- The person on the other end

## The habit worth keeping

Never share passwords, OTP codes or sensitive documents in any chat, encrypted or not. Encryption protects the pipe — not the people holding the ends.

## Quick recap
- Normal WhatsApp chats are end-to-end encrypted - not even WhatsApp can read them, so an AI cannot either.
- Meta AI only sees what you type directly to it in a Meta AI chat.
- If an app or AI tool asks for chat backups or notification access, refuse - that is how chats leak.
- Keep backups encrypted and never upload chat export files to unknown online tools.

One habit worth building: before forwarding anything sensitive to an AI chat, ask yourself if you would paste it into a public forum. The privacy question is the same, even though the chat feels private.
