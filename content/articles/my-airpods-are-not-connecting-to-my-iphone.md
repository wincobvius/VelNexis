You open the AirPods case near your iPhone and nothing pops up — no connection animation, no sound. Or they connect and then drop constantly. AirPods connection problems are extremely common and almost always solved by one of a few resets: the case trick, forgetting the device, or the full AirPods reset.

## How to fix it

1. Put AirPods in the case, close it, wait 15 seconds, open the lid near the iPhone.
2. Press and hold the button on the back of the case until the light blinks white.
3. Forget the device: Settings → Bluetooth → AirPods → Forget, then pair again.
4. Charge the case and AirPods for 30 minutes.
5. Reset AirPods: with lid open, hold the case button 15 seconds until amber then white.

## More things to try
- Put both AirPods in the case, keep the lid open, and hold the button on the back for about 15 seconds until the light flashes amber then white. This resets them to factory settings.
- On your iPhone go to Settings > Bluetooth, tap the 'i' next to your AirPods and choose 'Forget This Device'. Then pair again.
- Charge the case for 30 minutes first. A nearly dead case can fail to pair even when the light looks on.
- Toggle Airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off, then open the case near the phone.
- Check for an iOS update. Old software sometimes fails to see new AirPods firmware.
- If one AirPod connects and the other does not, the reset above usually fixes it.

Forget and re-pair both AirPods as a pair, never one at a time. Single-ear pairing attempts corrupt the link between the buds themselves, which then presents as a 'won't connect' problem that resets alone won't fix.
