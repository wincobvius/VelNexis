Your PC used to start in 30 seconds, now it takes 5 minutes; apps open slowly and everything lags. Slow computers are the number one computer complaint in the world. The causes: too many programs starting with Windows, a full hard drive, dust and heat, old hardware (especially a normal hard drive instead of an SSD), or too little RAM for today's software. Most slow PCs can be made fast again without buying anything.

## How to fix it

1. Restart the PC — many people never restart for weeks.
2. Press Ctrl + Shift + Esc to open Task Manager. End tasks you do not need.
3. Stop startup apps: Task Manager → Startup tab → disable heavy apps.
4. Delete unused programs: Settings → Apps.
5. If it is still slow with only 4 GB RAM, adding RAM or an SSD makes the biggest difference.

Q: What is the single biggest speed upgrade for an old PC?
A: Replacing the old hard drive with an SSD. It turns 5-minute starts into 30-second starts — nothing else comes close.

## The slow PC checklist
- HDD to SSD: the single fix that transforms old PCs. If it still has a spinning drive, this is the answer.
- RAM pinned at 100 percent: close tab-hoarding browsers, or add RAM - 8GB is the practical floor.
- Task Manager > Startup: disable everything unnecessary.
- Run a malware scan - miners quietly eat half the PC.
- Pending Windows updates slow everything while they finish in the background.
- Storage 95 percent full: Windows needs room to breathe - free 10 to 15 percent.
- Check temperatures - a throttled PC is a slow PC.
- Reset Windows with 'Keep my files' - the honest last step that fixes years of software rot in one hour.
