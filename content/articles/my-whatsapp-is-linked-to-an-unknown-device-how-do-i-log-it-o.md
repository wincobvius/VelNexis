In Linked Devices you see a computer, browser or device that is not yours — meaning someone else may be reading your chats in real time. This happens when someone scanned your QR code, or a hacker got access. The good news: you can see every linked device and kick them out from your phone in seconds.

## Evict them now

1. **WhatsApp → Settings → Linked Devices.** The list shows every computer, browser and companion app currently able to use your account, with its last active time.
2. **Tap the unknown device → Log out.** The eviction is instant and final — their session dies mid-scroll.
3. **Change your 2-step verification PIN** (Settings → Account → Two-step verification) so a removed device cannot easily re-link.
4. **Think about how they got in:** someone scanned your QR while holding your phone (check who had access), or you left WhatsApp Web open on a shared/public computer. The QR code only appears when YOU open Linked Devices → Link a Device — it cannot be stolen remotely.

## The audit habit

Open Linked Devices monthly and glance at the list. Anything you do not recognize — a browser you last used a year ago, a device named strangely — gets logged out without ceremony. It is the same hygiene as checking "where you're logged in" on any other account.

## If it keeps happening

A device that reappears after eviction means someone has physical access to your unlocked phone, or a companion app re-authorized itself. Factory-resetting the phone is the nuclear option; a strong screen lock and watching who handles your phone is the everyday one.
