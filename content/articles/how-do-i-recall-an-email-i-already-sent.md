You pressed Send — and instantly regretted it. Wrong attachment, wrong person, angry words written in haste. Can you pull it back? The honest answer: only for a very short time in Gmail, and only in special conditions in Outlook. But you can also set up Gmail so you always have a 30-second safety window for the future.

## How to fix it

1. Gmail: after sending, a small "Undo" appears at the bottom left for a few seconds. Click it fast.
2. To make Undo time longer: Gmail settings → General → Undo Send → set 30 seconds.
3. If more than 30 seconds passed, you cannot recall in Gmail.
4. Outlook with a work/school account has a real recall option: open the sent mail → File → Message resend/recall.
5. Recall only works when the other person has not opened the mail.

## The honest truth about recalling
- Real recall only works Outlook-to-Outlook inside one company, and only if the mail is unread. Gmail, Outlook.com and Yahoo have no true recall.
- In Outlook desktop: open the sent item in Sent Items > File > Resend or Recall. Success is never guaranteed.
- Gmail's Undo Send gives up to 30 seconds after pressing send - enable it in Settings > General, and set it to the maximum.
- What actually works later: send a short correction with the right attachment or number. 'Please ignore my previous mail' is normal business - people see it weekly.
- Sent something private to the wrong person? Reply quickly, ask them to delete it, and inform your manager if it was sensitive.
- Prevention beats recall: attach files first, add recipients last. Gmail even warns when you mention an attachment but forgot it.
- A delayed-send rule (1-2 minutes) in Outlook saves more mistakes than any recall ever will.
