Your phone storage is filling up and when you check, WhatsApp is using 5, 10 or even 20+ GB. This happens because every group photo, video and voice note is saved on your phone automatically — years of group forwards add up to huge amounts. WhatsApp has a built-in cleaning tool that most people never find.

## The cleanup

1. **Open the tool: WhatsApp → Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage.** It shows exactly what is eating space, sorted by size and by "forwarded many times" — the two views that matter.
2. **Delete the big and the forwarded first** — inside the tool you can select many items at once. A 90-second video from a group is 50+ photos' worth of storage; the tool surfaces them for you.
3. **Review groups one by one** (the tool lists them by storage used): the family group with 400 videos is a personal archive decision, the shopping-forward group is not.
4. **Turn off auto-download before anything else:** Settings → Storage and data → "Download to device" — set media to Wi-Fi only, or off entirely for the noisiest chats. Media still appears in chats; it just stops saving itself to your phone's storage permanently.

## Two settings worth knowing

- **Disappearing messages** on storage-heavy groups (open the group → name → Disappearing messages) auto-cleans content after the chosen window. For forward-storm groups, it is a firehose with a drain.
- Deleting media from WhatsApp's view also removes it from your phone's gallery copies saved by WhatsApp — if a video matters, save it deliberately before cleaning.

## The maintenance rhythm

Run Manage storage once a month. The first run frees gigabytes; every run after that takes two minutes.
