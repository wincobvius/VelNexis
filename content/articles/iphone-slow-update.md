You installed the latest iOS update and suddenly the phone that ran perfectly now lags — apps open slowly, the keyboard stutters, animations jump. This is one of the most reported iPhone complaints after every big update. There are two normal reasons: right after an update the phone re-organizes files for a day or two, and sometimes the new system is simply heavier for older models. Both have fixes.

## How to fix it

1. After a big update, the phone re-indexes files for 1–2 days. Wait it out.
2. Restart the iPhone: hold power + volume, slide to power off.
3. Free up storage — keep at least 5–10 GB free.
4. Turn off Background App Refresh for apps that do not need it: Settings → General.
5. If it stays slow for weeks, a reset with "keep data" or updating to the next iOS usually helps.

Q: Why does Apple make updates that slow old iPhones?
A: New software is built for new hardware — older chips get the same features but work harder for them. Apple also throttles very weak batteries; a battery replacement often brings the speed back.

## Speed up after the update
- Give it 2 to 3 days first - post-update indexing and background maintenance make every update look terrible, then settle.
- Check battery health in Settings > Battery: iOS deliberately slows iPhones with weak batteries. Under 80 percent means replacement.
- Free up storage - updates bloat caches and near-full storage drags everything.
- Restart once after the update to clear stuck background tasks.
- Turn off Background App Refresh for apps you rarely use.
- Reset All Settings (not content) fixes corrupted settings without data loss.
- One app slow after the update? That app's update is the problem - watch the App Store for the patch.
