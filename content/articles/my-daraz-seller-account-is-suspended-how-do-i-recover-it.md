Your Daraz seller account — your online shop — is suddenly suspended. You cannot receive orders, and your sales stop. Daraz suspends accounts for late shipping, fake or wrong products, too many customer complaints, or broken seller rules. Suspension is serious but not always final: Daraz has an appeal process, and sellers who fix the root cause often get the account back.

## How to fix it

1. Daraz emails the reason — usually late shipping, fake products, or many customer complaints.
2. Log in to Seller Center and check your violation list.
3. Write an appeal to Daraz seller support, admit the mistake and show how you fixed it.
4. Attach proof (delivery receipts, invoices) if the complaint was false.
5. If the account stays closed, you can register again carefully with new correct documents.

## Recover the seller account
- Read the suspension email fully - Daraz states the exact violation, like late shipping or wrong items. Your appeal must answer that specific point.
- Gather proof before appealing: tracking numbers, photos of packed items, and chat logs with buyers.
- Appeal through Seller Center support with a short, factual message. No blame and no long story - just facts and documents.
- Do not open a second seller account while suspended. It breaks the rules again and usually ends with both accounts gone for good.
- Fix the root cause at the same time - update listings, shipping times or stock records - so the same complaint does not repeat.
- Responses can take days to weeks. Keep one ticket open and updated instead of opening many new ones.

Open Seller Center's performance section and screenshot every metric before appealing. Appeals that quote the seller's own numbers read as professional; appeals that complain read as noise.
