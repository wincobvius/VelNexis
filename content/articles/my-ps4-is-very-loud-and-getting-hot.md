Your PS4 roars like a jet engine during games, and the top of the console is too hot to touch. The PS4's small fan fights years of collected dust; when vents clog and the thermal paste inside dries, the fan spins faster and louder trying to save the machine. Heat also throttles performance, so loud PS4s are also slow PS4s.

## How to fix it

1. Turn it off and unplug. Clean the vents with compressed air.
2. Dust inside the fan is the main cause — deep cleaning needs opening the case (many YouTube guides exist for your model).
3. Give it open space — never inside a closed cabinet.
4. Clean or replace the thermal paste if you are comfortable opening it — old paste causes loud fans.
5. If the noise is a loud whine from the disc drive, the drive may need service.

## Quiet the PS4
- The noise is the fan fighting heat. Move the PS4 into open air - not inside a closed cabinet, not touching other devices.
- Clean it: blow the dust out through the vents, or open the top cover and clean the fan and heatsink properly.
- Replace the thermal paste on a 5+ year old console - the single biggest fix for jet-engine PS4s.
- Keep the room cool; summer heat alone pushes PS4s into loud mode.
- A failing hard drive also whines - an SSD swap runs cooler and quieter.
- Loud fan plus sudden shutdowns means the overheat protection is working. Stop playing and service it before the board suffers.

Stand the PS4 vertically if you have the stand - the intake vents work noticeably better, and fan noise drops within minutes. Zero cost, real difference.
