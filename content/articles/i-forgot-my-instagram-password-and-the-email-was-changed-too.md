This is the worst Instagram situation — you do not remember the password AND the recovery email on the account is no longer yours (changed by a hacker, or you lost that old email). Normal recovery fails because the reset link goes to the wrong place. But Instagram keeps backup doors open: your phone number, Facebook login, or video selfie.

## Try the backup doors, in order

1. **Log in with your phone number** instead of the email — if your number is on the account, it works as the username.
2. **"Forgot password" → send the code to your phone** instead of email. A code by SMS resets everything, including the email on the account.
3. **Check the old email's "email changed" alert** if you still have any access to it — Instagram sends a confirmation when the account email changes, with a "revert this change" link. Hackers usually change the email first; this link undoes their first move.
4. **Your Facebook connection:** if the Instagram was linked to your Facebook account, log in via Facebook ("Log in with Facebook") and change the email/password from inside settings.

## If every door fails

5. **The video-selfie verification:** on the login help page, Instagram can ask for a short video selfie to confirm you are a real person matching the account's photos. It feels odd, but it is their official path for exactly this dead-end case.
6. **Still locked out?** Submit the "My account was hacked" form from Instagram's help center — and be persistent: from a device you used before, with consistent information, repeated honest attempts over days often succeed where one attempt fails.

## Afterwards

Recovery is only half the job: set a fresh email you control, add your phone number, and turn on two-factor authentication with backup codes saved — this time somewhere you will not lose.
