You cannot log in because the password no longer works, and the email on the account may have been changed by a hacker. Your photos, followers and messages are all still there — you just need to get back in. Instagram has several recovery doors: email, phone number, linked Facebook, and even a video selfie check when everything else fails.

## How to fix it

1. Open the Instagram login page → Forgot password.
2. Send the reset link to your email or phone number.
3. If the hacker changed the email, check your email inbox for the message "your email was changed" — click "revert this change" in it.
4. If nothing works, use the video selfie verification: Instagram login → "Try another way" → video selfie.
5. After recovery, turn on 2-factor authentication at once.

## Take the account back
- Try 'Forgot password' on the login screen first - if the hacker left your email or phone number attached, you reset it in one minute.
- Use Instagram's own 'My account was hacked' help page. It walks you through identity verification, including a photo of yourself holding a code.
- Secure your email first - hackers often start there. Change that password and turn on 2FA before anything else.
- If another device is still logged in, change the password from there and log out all other sessions.
- Revoke third-party apps in Settings > Apps and websites - old granted apps are a common way in.
- If the account posts scam links, warn followers quickly - their reports speed up support.
- Once back in, turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app, not SMS.

Check the 'Email' field in your own profile after recovery - hackers often change it to theirs, which lets them walk back in. Restore your email first, then everything else.
