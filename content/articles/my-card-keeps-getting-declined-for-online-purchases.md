Your card works in shops but online payments keep declining. The decline message never tells you the reason, which is the real problem — but the cause always lives in one of three places: the card itself, your bank's settings, or the merchant's payment system. This order checks all three, cheapest first.

## Check the card side

1. **Active and unexpired?** Check the expiry date and, for a new card, whether it needs activation first.
2. **Funds or credit available?** Include the site's currency conversion — a payment that looks within budget can exceed your limit after conversion.
3. **Typed correctly?** Card number, expiry, CVV, and the name exactly as printed on the card. One transposed digit is the oldest decline in banking.

## Check the bank side

4. **Ask the bank to enable e-commerce on the card.** Many banks keep online and international transactions switched OFF by default — this is the single most common cause of "works in shops, fails online". The bank app often has the switch under card settings; otherwise one call: *"enable e-commerce transactions on my card."*
5. **Ask for the specific decline reason** from the helpline. Their system shows exactly why each attempt failed — limit, currency, merchant category — which ends the guessing instantly.

## Check the merchant side

6. **Only one site declining?** Then the problem is usually theirs — their payment processor, their fraud filters, or a mismatch with your card type. Try another card there, and your card on another site; the pattern identifies the culprit.
