You applied for YouTube monetization and got rejected for "reused content" — meaning YouTube believes your videos are built from other people's material. For compilations, clip channels and AI-voiceover channels, this is the most common rejection. YouTube wants content where your own contribution is obvious and transformational.

## What "reused" means to a reviewer

Not just "stolen" — a human reviewer watches samples of your channel and asks: if we removed the parts you did not make, would anything remain? Rejections go to:
- Compilations of others' clips joined by captions
- Movie/TV clips with background music or minimal commentary
- AI-voice readings of text (news articles, Reddit threads) over stock footage
- Re-uploads of your OWN content repeated across channels with no new value

## What counts as transformation

1. **Original commentary and analysis:** reacting with substance, explaining, adding context — your voice and judgment AS the content, with the source material as illustration.
2. **Original visuals:** filming your own segments, your own graphics, your own edits — even covering others' topics with genuinely made-by-you presentation.
3. **Documented permission:** licensed footage with rights shown, Creative Commons material used within its terms — YouTube's forms allow submitting evidence of rights.
4. **The honest self-test:** could a viewer describe what makes YOUR video different from the raw material? If the honest answer is "the title", the channel is what YouTube's policy describes.

## The recovery path

5. **Do not delete the flagged videos and reapply instantly** — reviewers look at the channel's overall pattern. First, transform or delete the weakest content: videos with no original contribution go private (not just unlisted).
6. **Build 4-6 weeks of genuinely original work** before reapplying — a visible direction change, not a cosmetic one.
7. **At reapplication, the appeal field matters:** state plainly what changed — "removed compilation content; new videos feature my own commentary and original footage." Reviewers read it; template appeals are ignored.
8. **Check the other reasons on the same email** — reused content often arrives alongside "repetitious content" (mass-produced samey videos). Both need fixing, and fixing one leaves the rejection standing.
