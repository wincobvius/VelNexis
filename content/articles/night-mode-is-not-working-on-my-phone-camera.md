Your phone is supposed to have night mode — that feature that makes dark photos bright and clear — but it never activates. Night mode is picky by design: it only turns on in genuinely low light, needs a steady phone, and can be disabled in settings. Older phones simply do not have it at all.

## How to fix it

1. Night mode turns on automatically only in low light — check you are in a dark place.
2. Keep the phone very still for the full countdown — movement cancels it.
3. Check the mode is not disabled in camera settings.
4. Clean the lens — night mode needs light to work, and dirt blocks it.
5. Older phones may not have night mode at all — check your model's features.

## Make night mode work
- Night mode only turns on in real darkness and needs 2 to 5 seconds per shot - hold the phone still or rest it on something.
- Clean the lens with a soft cloth. Night shots turn every smudge into a glow.
- Turn off beauty filters or HDR - on many phones they fight with night mode and it never triggers.
- Tap your subject to focus, then slide the brightness (sun icon) down a little. Overexposed night shots look foggy.
- If the built-in mode is weak, try the night mode in Google Camera app ports.
- For photos of people at night, ask them to hold still for the full capture - the countdown on screen tells you how long.

Brace the phone against a wall or table for night shots - night mode multiplies every tiny hand movement into blur. A steady surface improves night photos more than any third-party app.
