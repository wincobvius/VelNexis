Your phone went into water and now will not start, or works strangely. Inside, water is quietly corroding the delicate board — and every hour matters. The question underneath: is the data (photos, chats, contacts) still there? The answer is usually hopeful, because storage chips survive water better than the rest of the phone.

## How to fix it

1. Do not charge it or press the power button — this causes short circuits.
2. Take it to a repair shop as soon as possible. Technicians clean the board with special solutions.
3. The faster you go, the better the chance — corrosion starts within hours.
4. Data usually survives even when the phone dies, because storage chips are separate.
5. Do not put it in rice and wait — that wastes the critical first hours.

## Do and don't after water
- Do NOT charge it and do NOT try to turn it on. Electricity plus water inside is what actually kills the phone.
- Skip the rice myth - it does nothing that open air does not. Leave the phone with the SIM tray out to dry in moving air for 48 hours.
- If it was seawater or soapy water, it needs opening and cleaning quickly - salt eats the board within days.
- If photos matter most, your cloud backup (Google Photos or iCloud) may already hold everything - check from another device before paying for recovery.
- A dead water phone can often be revived by a repair shop board cleaning. Data recovery from the chip is possible but costs more.
- If it turns on after drying, back up everything immediately - water damage can come back weeks later.
