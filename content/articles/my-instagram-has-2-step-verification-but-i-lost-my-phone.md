Your Instagram is protected with 2-step verification (a good thing!), but your phone is lost, stolen or dead — and the security codes were coming to that phone. Now you cannot log in even with the right password. This feels like being locked out of your own house, but Instagram keeps backup methods for exactly this situation.

## The backup doors, in order

1. **Backup codes** — the codes Instagram told you to save when you enabled 2-step verification. One code logs you in directly, no phone needed. If you saved them (in a notebook, a password manager, printed), this is a 30-second fix.
2. **"Try another way" on the login screen** — Instagram cycles through every other verification method on the account: your email address, or a code via WhatsApp/SMS to a recovery number.
3. **Your new phone, same number:** if you kept your SIM (replacement SIM from your operator with the same number), the SMS codes simply arrive on the new phone.
4. **Nothing works?** The identity-verification path: Instagram's login help can ask for a video selfie or documents — slow but real, and it exists precisely for this dead end.

## Once you are in

- Change the password and review the 2-step settings: add your CURRENT phone number, confirm your email, and regenerate backup codes — save them somewhere that survives phone loss this time (a password manager or paper).
- While you are at it, check "Devices and login activity" for anything unfamiliar.

## The lesson without the lockout

Backup codes are not optional decoration. Save them the day you enable 2-step verification — every one of these rescue paths exists because someone skipped that step.
