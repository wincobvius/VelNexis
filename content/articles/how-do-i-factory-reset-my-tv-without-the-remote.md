You need to factory reset your TV — but the remote is lost, broken, or its buttons refuse to respond. The available reset method depends on your TV's brand and model, so try these routes in order and use whichever your set supports.

> **Before you start:** a factory reset erases the TV's settings, Wi-Fi logins, apps and tuned channels. Note down anything you will want to re-enter afterwards.

## Route 1 — the hidden physical buttons

Many TVs hide a small button or joystick on the back, the bottom edge, or under the front logo. A long press often opens the menu — and on some models performs the reset directly. Your model's manual shows its exact spot.

## Route 2 — your phone as the remote

1. Android TVs: the Google TV app (Android/iOS) can control the TV over Wi-Fi without pairing.
2. Other brands: Samsung TVs often work with the SmartThings app; LG and others have their own remote apps.
3. This needs the TV and phone on the same Wi-Fi network — which works if the TV is still connected.

## Route 3 — button combinations

With the TV unplugged, some models reset when you hold the physical power button while plugging power back in, or hold a brand-specific combination during boot. Search your exact model number plus "factory reset without remote" for the right sequence.

## If nothing works

A cheap universal remote that lists your brand — or the TV maker's support line — solves this without repair costs.

Before resetting, try the TV maker's phone app once - most modern TVs can be fully controlled over WiFi, and that includes navigating to the reset menu. It turns a no-remote emergency into a five-minute job.
