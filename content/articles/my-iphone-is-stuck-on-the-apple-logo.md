You turn on the iPhone, the Apple logo appears — and stays there forever, or the phone shows the logo, goes black, shows it again, in an endless loop. This happens after failed updates, interrupted restores, full storage, or (rarely) hardware failure. The forced restart combination solves a big share of these cases in under a minute.

## How to fix it

1. Force restart: press volume up, press volume down, then hold the side button until the logo returns.
2. If it loops again, connect to a computer and update iOS through iTunes/Finder (update keeps data).
3. If update fails, restore (this erases data — only if you have a backup).
4. Charge the phone 30 minutes first — a very low battery can cause the loop.
5. If nothing works, visit Apple service.

## Fix the boot loop
- Force restart first: press volume up, press volume down, then hold the side button until the phone restarts.
- If it loops back to the logo, connect the phone to a computer with a cable.
- Open iTunes or Finder (or the Apple Devices app on Windows) - if the phone is detected, choose Update first. This reinstalls the software without deleting your data.
- If Update fails, the only remaining option is Restore, which erases the phone. Do it only if you have a backup, or accept the loss.
- Boot loops right after a battery or screen repair usually mean a loose connector - reopen and reseat the flex cables.
- A completely full storage is a common trigger for boot loops - the Update path above still works.
- Ignore 'boot loop fixer' software from ads. iTunes or Finder Update is the only safe repair.
