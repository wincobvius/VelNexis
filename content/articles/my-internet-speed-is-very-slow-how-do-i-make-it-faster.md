Videos keep loading, downloads crawl, and pages take forever — even though you pay for a good package. Slow internet has two kinds: the company is giving less speed than promised, or your home setup is wasting the speed you have. Walls, router position, old routers, and too many connected devices are the most common home-side causes. Testing the real speed tells you which side the problem is on.

## How to fix it

1. Test your speed on fast.com. Compare it with your package speed.
2. Restart the router.
3. Move the router to the middle of the house, high up, away from walls.
4. Disconnect devices you are not using. Someone may be downloading.
5. If speed is always lower than your package, call your internet company.

## Simple checks first
- Restart the router first. Unplug it for 30 seconds. This alone fixes slow internet more often than anything else.
- Stand next to the router and run a speed test (fast.com). If it is fast there but slow in your room, the problem is WiFi range, not your connection.
- Stop background downloads - Windows updates, cloud backups, and other people streaming in the same house all eat speed.
- Test with a cable if you can. If the cable is fast but WiFi is slow, focus on WiFi placement or a mesh unit.
- Change the DNS on your device to 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8. It is free and often opens pages faster.
- If nothing helps, call your provider and ask them to check the line. Also ask a neighbor on the same provider if their speed is bad too.

Run one speed test at the router and one in the far room, same hour. The gap between those two numbers is the entire diagnosis: big gap means WiFi placement, small gap means the line itself.

Slow internet is a diagnosis job, not a settings job. Match what you notice to the likely cause before changing anything.

## Match the symptom to the cause

| What you notice | Most likely cause | First move |
| Slow only at night | Congestion on the shared line | Test at 9am and 9pm, show both to the provider |
| Slow on one device only | That device's WiFi chip or settings | Restart it, then forget and rejoin the network |
| Slow everywhere, always | Line quality or plan limit | Compare your plan speed with the router's own stats |
| Fast speed test, slow browsing | DNS delays | Switch DNS to 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8 |
