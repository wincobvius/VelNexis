The same dark spot or shadow appears in every photo, in the same place, no matter the scene. This means something is physically between the world and the sensor: dirt under the lens glass, debris inside the lens module, or a damaged sensor. The white-wall test confirms it in one shot.

## How to fix it

1. Clean the lens first — dirt inside the case or on the lens makes spots.
2. Take a photo of a plain white wall — if the spot stays in the same place, it is inside the lens.
3. Dust inside the lens needs service — the module is opened and cleaned.
4. Check for a cracked lens cover — replace the glass.
5. If the spot only appears in some apps, it is software, not hardware.

## Find where the spot lives
- Clean the back lens with a soft cloth first - a fingerprint or dust speck is the most common 'black spot'.
- Take one photo of a plain white wall or the sky. Then cover the lens fully with your finger and take another. If the spot appears in both, it is a sensor problem, not dirt.
- Switch between lenses (1x, 2x, wide). If the spot only appears on one lens, you know which lens is affected.
- Try a different camera app. If the spot shows in every app, it is hardware.
- Sensor dust needs professional cleaning; dead pixels on a phone camera usually mean replacing the camera module.
- If the phone is under warranty, take the test photos with you - they prove the fault instantly.

Take one photo of a plain white wall in good light - the spot test. Sharp dark spot means lens or sensor debris; soft dark patch means sensor damage. The wall photo is also the evidence a repair shop needs.
