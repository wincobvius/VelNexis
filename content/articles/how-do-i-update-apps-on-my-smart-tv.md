Your TV apps are outdated — new features missing, old bugs annoying you — and you want to update them. TVs differ by brand here: Samsung, LG and Android TV each hide updates in their own store. Most TVs also have auto-update, which is usually switched on but worth verifying.

## By brand

- **Samsung:** Apps (Smart Hub) → Settings (gear icon) → Updates → Update All. Auto-update lives in the same place.
- **LG:** LG Content Store → App list → each app shows an Update button; the store's settings hold automatic updates.
- **Android TV / Google TV:** the Play Store on the TV → profile icon → Manage apps and games → Updates. Auto-update: Play Store settings → Auto-update apps → "Auto-update at any time".
- **Others (Hisense, TCL and similar):** usually Android TV rules or an app store icon on the home bar with an updates section.

## Why an app will not update

1. **Full TV storage** — the quiet blocker: Settings → Storage. A TV packed with unused apps refuses updates with no clear error. See our TV storage guide for the cleanup.
2. **The app version on your TV is abandoned** — very old TVs stop receiving app updates entirely (the maker stopped supporting the TV's software). The app still runs its last version; the fix is a streaming stick carrying current apps.
3. **Updates "installed" but the app behaves the same** — a restart of the TV finishes the swap properly.

## Worth knowing

- The TV's OWN software (firmware) updates separately from apps, and apps sometimes require a newer firmware: Settings → Support/About → Software Update. Do both.
- Auto-update is worth leaving ON for a TV — manual updates on a slow TV remote are nobody's hobby.
