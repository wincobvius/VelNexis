One or more vertical lines — colored or white — run down your TV screen from top to bottom. Lines mean trouble inside the display itself: the panel, its cable connections, or the board driving it. The critical test: whether the lines appear on the TV's own menu screen (hardware) or only on certain inputs (external).

## How to fix it

1. Restart the TV and check different inputs.
2. Gently press on the screen edge far from the lines — loose panel cables sometimes respond (be very gentle).
3. If lines appear on the TV's own menu screen too, the panel is damaged — panel replacement often costs near a new TV; get quotes.
4. If lines appear only on one HDMI device, that device or cable is the problem, not the TV.
5. Lines after the TV was hit or pressed confirm panel damage.

## Fix the lines
- Fixed line in one place = panel or driver fault. Moving lines = signal or cable fault.
- Reseat the HDMI cable at both ends, then try another cable and another port.
- Test the TV's own apps like YouTube. Lines on apps too = panel; only on HDMI = cable or source.
- Unplug the TV for 10 minutes - a full power drain clears temporary glitches.
- Lightly press near the line: if it changes, an internal ribbon cable is loose - a technician reseats it cheaply.
- Lines after a hit or fall usually mean a cracked panel, and panels cost most of a TV's price.
- Under warranty? Photograph the lines and claim it now.

Press gently near the line with the TV off - if the line shifts or flickers when pressure moves, a ribbon cable is loose and a technician can reseat it cheaply. Fixed lines that ignore pressure point at the panel.
