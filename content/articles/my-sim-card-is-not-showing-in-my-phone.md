The phone shows "No SIM card" or "No service" even though the SIM is inside. You cannot call, text or use mobile data. This happens when the SIM is not sitting properly in its slot, the metal part of the SIM is dirty or scratched, the SIM is old and dead, or the phone's SIM reader is damaged. It also happens after a phone drop or after water damage. First find out if the problem is the SIM or the phone.

## How to fix it

1. Turn the phone off. Take the SIM out and put it back properly.
2. Clean the SIM metal part softly with a dry cloth.
3. Put the SIM in another phone. If it works there, your phone's SIM slot has a problem.
4. Restart the phone.
5. If nothing works, get a new SIM from your network shop with your ID.

## Get the SIM recognized
- Power off, take the SIM out, check the notch orientation, place it flat, and restart.
- Wipe the gold contacts with a dry cloth - dust and fingerprints block reading.
- Test the SIM in another phone. Works there = your phone's slot; dead there = SIM or network.
- Test another SIM in your phone to confirm which side failed.
- Check the IMEI in Settings > About phone - a blank or zero IMEI is a modem failure, service needed.
- Bent pins inside the slot, from forcing the tray, need a technician.
- SIMs inactive for months get recycled by networks - call your provider to reactivate before blaming hardware.

Test the SIM in another phone before anything else. Five seconds, and it splits the problem cleanly in half - phone or SIM - which saves you from fixing the wrong side.
