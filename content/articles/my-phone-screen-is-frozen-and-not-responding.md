The screen is stuck — touching it does nothing, buttons do not work, and you cannot swipe or close anything. This happens when the phone's memory is overloaded, an app has stopped responding, or the software crashed for a moment. Sometimes the touch itself is damaged if the phone fell or water went inside. A frozen screen looks scary but is usually fixed with a simple forced restart in seconds.

## How to fix it

1. Press and hold the power button for 15–30 seconds. The phone will restart.
2. If it does not, hold Power + Volume Down together for 10 seconds.
3. If the screen works after restart, close heavy apps.
4. If the screen freezes again and again, free up storage and update the phone.
5. If the screen never responds, the touch panel may be damaged. Go to a repair shop.

## Unfreeze the phone
- Hold the power button 10 to 20 seconds - almost every phone force-restarts this way (iPhone: volume up, volume down, then hold power).
- If the screen responds but one app is stuck, close that app from the recent apps view.
- Try the buttons with the charger plugged in - a frozen screen on an empty battery ignores presses.
- Screen black but the phone vibrates on calls? The screen or its cable failed, often after a drop. That is a service job.
- Frozen at the logo is a boot problem, not a frozen screen - different fix.
- Freezing in one app only: clear its cache and check its updates.
- Repeating freezes on an old phone: free storage and check battery health - both freeze whole systems.

Before assuming screen death, try the touch with a different finger or after washing your hands - wet fingers and cracked screen protectors both kill touch response and both are free to fix.
