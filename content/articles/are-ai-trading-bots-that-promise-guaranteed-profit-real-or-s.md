Ads promise a "smart AI robot" that trades crypto or forex for you — "deposit, relax, and earn daily profit, guaranteed." The promise itself is the problem: no legitimate investment anywhere offers guaranteed daily profit. Real markets move up and down, and something that cannot lose is not investing — it is the hook of a scam.

## What is actually documented

Plenty of apps and websites market themselves this way, and the pattern reported by victims is consistent: the dashboard shows growing profits, a small first withdrawal sometimes works to build trust, and larger withdrawals get blocked — with "account managers" pushing you to deposit more to "upgrade" or "unlock" your money.

## Before you put in anything

1. Check the regulator. Real investment services are registered with financial authorities (SECP in Pakistan, or the equivalent body elsewhere). Most of these bot apps are registered nowhere.
2. Treat "guaranteed daily profit" as the whole answer — that one phrase separates investing from scamming.
3. Never deposit savings or borrowed money. If a loss would hurt, the "opportunity" is not worth testing.
4. Already in? Try withdrawing everything now, stop depositing, and screenshot every transaction and chat as evidence.

## If money is already gone

Report to your bank immediately (recent transfers can sometimes be recalled), and to your national cybercrime service — in Pakistan, the FIA cybercrime wing (1991). Recovery is never guaranteed, which is exactly why the checks above matter more than the cure.

Q: Are all AI trading tools scams?
A: No — automated trading software genuinely exists and is used by professional firms. But those tools do not promise guaranteed profit to strangers through ads and WhatsApp groups. The promise is the scam signal, not the word "AI".
