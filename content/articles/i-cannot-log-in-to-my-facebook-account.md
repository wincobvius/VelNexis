You type your email/phone and password but Facebook says the password is wrong, or the account seems to have vanished. This happens after forgetting the password, after a hack that changed your details, or when Facebook suspects someone broke in and locks the login for safety. Facebook has an official account recovery flow designed exactly for this.

## The recovery flow

1. **Go to facebook.com/login/identify** — the "find your account" page. Enter the email or phone number on the account.
2. **Choose a recovery channel** — a code to your email or your phone. Enter it when it arrives.
3. **Set a new password** — one you use nowhere else. At this point, most "cannot log in" cases are solved.
4. **Recovery email or phone no longer yours?** (a hacker changed them, or you lost the old email) — click **"No longer have access to these?"** in the recovery flow. Facebook then offers its longer identity-verification path: recognizing friends from photos, or uploading an ID. From a device you used before, this succeeds far more often.
5. **"Account locked for security" message rather than a password error?** Follow the security check it presents — usually confirming your identity and recent activity — instead of resetting.

## After you are back in

Check Settings → Security and Login: every logged-in device (end unfamiliar sessions), your contact email and phone, and turn on two-factor authentication. And if the login broke because you typed a saved-but-old password, update it in your password manager — the next login attempt will thank you.

Try logging in from a device and browser you used before - Facebook's security trusts familiar combinations and skips several verification steps that fresh devices trigger.
