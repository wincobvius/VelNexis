There is no sound at all — no YouTube audio, no notification sounds — or the sound icon shows a cross. This happens when the wrong output device is selected (Windows sometimes sends sound to a disconnected HDMI monitor), the volume is muted, the audio driver crashed, or Windows updates broke the driver. It is one of the most common problems after Windows updates.

## How to fix it

1. Click the speaker icon — check it is not muted and volume is up.
2. Right click the speaker icon → Sound settings → choose the correct output device (speakers, not HDMI).
3. Restart the PC.
4. Update the sound driver: Device Manager → Sound → right click → Update driver.
5. Run the audio troubleshooter: Settings → Sound → Troubleshoot.

## Get sound back step by step
- Right-click the speaker icon > Sound settings and check the output device. After updates, Windows often switches to a monitor or HDMI device with no speakers.
- Open Volume Mixer while audio plays and look for a muted app.
- Run the audio troubleshooter (right-click the speaker icon > Troubleshoot). It fixes most driver glitches.
- Device Manager > Sound > your device > Uninstall, then restart - Windows installs a clean driver again.
- Unplug HDMI cables and test - HDMI takes over the sound even when the monitor has no speakers.
- If you use a USB headset or Bluetooth speaker, reconnect it and set it as default while it is actually connected.

## Quick recap
- Check the output device first - Windows switches it after updates.
- Volume Mixer exposes a muted app when the master volume looks fine.
- Uninstall the audio driver in Device Manager, then restart.
- HDMI silently steals sound to a monitor with no speakers.
