WhatsApp messages, emails and other alerts arrive hours late or never show on the lock screen. You only see them when you open the app yourself. This happens when notifications for that app are turned off, a battery saver is killing the app in the background, or Data Saver is blocking it. It is a settings problem, not a hardware fault, and it is fixed app by app.

## How to fix it

1. Settings → Apps → the app → Notifications → turn them ON.
2. Check that Data Saver or Battery Saver is not blocking the app.
3. Lock the app in recent apps so the system does not kill it.
4. Settings → Battery → turn off "restrict background activity" for that app.
5. Restart the phone.

## Check these one by one
- Turn off Do Not Disturb completely and test. Also check the phone is not in Battery Saver - it blocks notifications to save power.
- Open Settings > Apps > [the app] > Notifications and make sure the main toggle is on, not just some sub-options.
- On Android, go to the app's battery settings and set it to 'Unrestricted'. 'Optimized' apps often stop receiving push messages.
- Restart the phone. It sounds too simple, but it restarts the notification service too.
- Clear the app's cache (Settings > Apps > Storage > Clear cache).
- If only one app is silent, its notification categories may be muted - tap 'Categories' inside its notification settings and check each one.

## Quick recap
- Do Not Disturb and Battery Saver first - they block more notifications than any bug.
- Set the app to 'Unrestricted' battery on Android.
- Check per-category notification channels for the silent app.
- Restart after all settings changes - it refreshes the push service.
