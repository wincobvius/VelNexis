Ads explode everywhere — pop ups over pages, and the worst kind: notifications sliding in from the corner even when the browser is closed. Most of these "browser ads" are actually notification permissions you granted by clicking "Allow" on a sneaky popup. The rest are missing ad-blocking and adware. All three are fixable.

## Fix 1 — the notification flood (ads even with browser closed)

1. **Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Site Settings → Notifications.** You will see the list of sites you "allowed" — most of them disguised their notification request as a fake play button or "click to continue" wall. Remove every site you do not consciously want, and set the default to "Don't allow sites to send notifications". On the phone: Chrome → Settings → Site settings → Notifications, same purge.
2. This one setting ends the corner-of-screen ads that people blame on "virus" for months. They were permissions all along.

## Fix 2 — the on-page ad explosion

3. **Install uBlock Origin** (one, good, and enough — see our ad-blocking guide) and let it clean the pages themselves. Pop-ups over content, autoplaying video ads, mid-article interruptions: gone.
4. **Enable the browser's own blockers:** Chrome Settings → Privacy → "Block third-party cookies" and pop-up blocking (usually on by default — verify nothing turned them off).

## Fix 3 — the adware case (blocker installed, ads persist)

5. Ads redirecting you, injected into every page, plus a suspiciously slow PC: that is adware on the machine, not the browser. Full scan with Windows Security + a Malwarebytes pass, then uninstall unknown programs (Settings → Apps, sorted by date). See our browser-hijacking guide for the shortcut trick if redirects remain.
