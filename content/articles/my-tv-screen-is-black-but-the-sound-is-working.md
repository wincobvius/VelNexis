Your TV plays sound perfectly — dialogue, music, everything — but the screen is completely black. The TV is technically alive; only the picture is dead. The most common cause is failed backlight LEDs (the lights that shine through the screen): a very common, repairable fault. A simple flashlight test tells you in seconds whether the TV is worth repairing.

## How to fix it

1. Shine a flashlight on the screen — if you see a faint picture, the backlight is dead.
2. Backlight repair is a common and affordable TV repair — a technician changes the LED strips.
3. First unplug the TV for 2 minutes and replug — sometimes it resets.
4. Try another input/HDMI to rule out the source device.
5. If the screen is totally dark even with light, the panel or main board is the issue — get a repair quote before buying new.

## Fix black screen with sound
- The TV works - the panel or backlight does not. Flashlight test in a dark room: a faint picture visible under the light means the backlight failed - a moderate, repairable cost.
- Full power drain first: unplug 10 minutes, hold the power button 30 seconds, replug.
- Reseat and swap HDMI cables and ports - a broken handshake copies panel failure.
- Test the TV's own apps. Black there too = panel, not your device.
- Check picture settings - some energy saving modes dim to black.
- Backlight failure is repairable; a dead panel often costs near a new TV. Get a quote if it is under 5 years old.

Do the flashlight test in a dark room before calling anyone - shine a light at the screen and look for a faint image. If you see one, the panel is fine and the backlight failed, which is a far cheaper repair.
