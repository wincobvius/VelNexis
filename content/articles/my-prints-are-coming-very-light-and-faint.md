Your prints come out pale and washed out — text so light it strains the eyes, colors barely visible. Light prints point to three causes: economy mode secretly on, fading ink, or clogged heads (inkjet) / low toner (laser). The mode setting surprises everyone — printers turn it on silently to "save ink".

## The cheap check first

1. **Print preferences → the quality/mode settings:** if "Draft", "Economy" or "Ink Saver" is selected, you found it — printers and their software flip this on quietly. Set Normal quality and test again. One page settles it.
2. **The driver's "toner/ink save" checkbox:** some makers put a second saver in the printer's own software (Printer Assistant/Utility) — check both places, computer AND printer menus.

## Inkjet: the clogged head ladder

3. **Run the printer's own cleaning cycle** (its menu or software: "Clean Printhead" / "Nozzle Check"). Print a nozzle check page first — broken lines in the pattern = clogged nozzles. Clean once, test, clean again — up to 2-3 cycles. Cleaning uses ink, so stop at three.
4. **Manually clogged (idle months):** a printhead that sat dry for months needs deeper help — printer shops do ultrasonic/manual cleaning cheaply; on printers with removable heads, isopropyl and a lint-free cloth on the contact plate is the DIY version (search your exact model — some heads must not be touched).
5. **Genuine low ink:** ink level gauges are estimates, not measurements — a cartridge showing "some left" can print faint. Swap in a fresh cartridge to test. (Third-party/refilled cartridges print faint more often than genuine — a cost/quality tradeoff to know.)

## Laser: the toner ladder

6. **Low toner:** remove the cartridge and rock it gently side-to-side 5-6 times (level, not shaking like a cocktail) — this redistributes remaining toner and buys 50-100 more pages. Faint returns = genuinely low: replace.
7. **"Econo/toner save" mode** is even more common on lasers — check the driver and the printer's menu.
8. **Damp or wrong paper** absorbs toner badly — fresh dry paper rules out the medium before buying toner.

## The sudden-change red flag

Prints that went faint AFTER a cartridge swap may have a protective tape/cover left on the new cartridge — the classic. Pull it back out and look.
