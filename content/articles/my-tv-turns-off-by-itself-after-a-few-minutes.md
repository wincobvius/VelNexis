Your TV switches itself off after minutes of viewing — sometimes back on, sometimes staying dead until you press power. Self-turning-off TVs have two families of causes: settings (sleep timers, eco modes) and hardware (failing power boards, overheating). The blink pattern of the standby light after shutdown is the TV telling you which family it is.

## How to fix it

1. Check the sleep timer: Settings → System/Sleep timer → OFF.
2. Check eco/power saving mode — it can turn the TV off.
3. Check the power socket and cable — a loose plug cuts power.
4. Check if it turns off only on one input/app — that device may be the problem.
5. If it turns off and the standby light blinks in a pattern, the TV is reporting a fault — that blink code identifies the failed board (search your model + blink code).

## Stop the random shutdowns
- Check the sleep or off timer in Settings > System or General > Timer - a set timer explains fixed-minute shutdowns.
- Unplug for 10 minutes to clear control-board glitches.
- HDMI-CEC: one misbehaving stick or box can power the TV off. Turn CEC off and test.
- Overheating: wall-mounted TVs flat against a hot wall, or enclosed cabinets, overheat and shut down. Check the vents.
- Reseat the power cable and try another wall socket.
- If it restarts to the logo instead of staying off, update the firmware.
- Aging TVs that shut off more over the years usually have failing power-board capacitors - a cheap fix at any TV shop.

Count the minutes before shutdown - exactly the same interval every time means a timer or setting, random intervals mean heat or power. That one observation halves the troubleshooting.
