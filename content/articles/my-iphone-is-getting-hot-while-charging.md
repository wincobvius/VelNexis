While charging, your iPhone becomes noticeably hot — warm is normal, but this is uncomfortable to hold. Causes: thick cases trapping heat, fast charging's natural warmth, using the phone heavily while plugged in, or charging in hot places. Rarely, constant extreme heat signals a battery problem needing service.

## Separate normal from not-normal

- **Warm while fast-charging from low battery** → normal chemistry; fast charging pushes power quickly and the phone warms.
- **Hot + charging + screen-on gaming/video** → you are using and filling the battery simultaneously; the phone heats from both sides. Unplug or stop the heavy use — one of the two, not both.
- **Hot with a thick case on, every session** → the case is the blanket. Take it off while charging.
- **Hot even when idle on a slow charger, or hot around the camera area constantly** → that pattern deserves the checks below.

## The cooling routine

1. **Remove the case while charging**, especially overnight or with fast chargers.
2. **Charge away from heat:** off the bed, off sunlight through a window, off other warm electronics.
3. **Use a slower charger before bed** — overnight charging needs no speed, and gentle charging keeps temperatures low.
4. **Close the heavy apps** before charging — navigation, video, games keep the processor working while the battery fills.
5. **Update iOS** — some heat bugs are software and Apple patches them.

## When to stop and get it looked at

If the phone is hot while NOT charging and NOT being used, or the heat sits in one spot constantly, or the battery health number is dropping fast — stop using third-party chargers, and take it to a service center. A swelling phone (screen lifting) turns off and goes to service immediately, not in a drawer. Charging heat is usually habits; constant heat is hardware.
