You cannot remember your iPhone passcode, and after several wrong attempts the phone disables itself — finally showing "iPhone unavailable". Apple's security here is absolute: there is no trick, no backdoor, no way to see your data without the passcode. The only path forward erases the phone — and your iCloud backup brings your life back afterwards.

## How to fix it

1. Too many wrong attempts lock the phone and finally erase it (if erase after attempts is on).
2. You must erase the iPhone to remove the passcode — no way around it.
3. Put the iPhone in recovery mode: connect to a computer, then press volume up, volume down, then hold side button until the recovery screen shows.
4. On the computer open iTunes (or Finder on Mac) → Restore.
5. After restore, set up the phone and log in with your Apple ID to get data back from iCloud backup.

## Unlock a forgotten passcode
- The only way in is a wipe - Apple designed it so nobody else can get in either.
- If it trusted a computer before, connect and open iTunes or Finder - a backup still works while locked. Do that first.
- Then recovery mode: volume up, volume down, hold power until the connect-to-computer screen, then Restore.
- icloud.com/find can erase it remotely if the phone has internet.
- After the wipe, the phone asks for the Apple ID that was on it - that password must be known.
- Restore from iCloud backup during setup - photos return if backup was on.
- If the Apple ID password is lost too, start Apple's account recovery first. It can take days, but it is the only road.
