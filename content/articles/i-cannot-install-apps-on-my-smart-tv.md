You want new apps on your Smart TV — streaming services, games — but the app store refuses: apps missing, installs failing, or storage full errors. TVs have tiny storage (often 4–8 GB) and region-locked app stores, and older TVs lose app support entirely. Knowing your TV's limits points to the right workaround.

## How to fix it

1. Check your TV's app store — some TVs only support their own store.
2. Check free storage on the TV: Settings → Apps — full TVs cannot install.
3. Restart the TV and try again.
4. Check the app is available in your country — some apps are region locked.
5. Old TVs lose app support over time — if the store no longer offers the app, a streaming stick (Chromecast, Fire TV) is the cheap fix.

## Install apps on your TV
- First check which app store your TV has: Samsung (Smart Hub), LG (Content Store), Android and Google TV (Play Store), TCL and others (Roku or Android).
- If the app is missing from the store, your TV model or region may not support it. The maker's website lists supported apps per model.
- Update the TV software first (Settings > Support > Software Update) - old firmware hides newer apps.
- Not enough storage? Delete unused apps - TV storage is tiny and full storage blocks installs.
- If the store itself will not open, check the TV's date, time and internet, then unplug the TV for a full minute.
- A cheap Chromecast or streaming stick adds every app to any TV with an HDMI port - often the best fix for old TVs.

Check the TV's storage before anything else - smart TVs ship with tiny storage, and a handful of apps fills it. Deleting two unused apps often fixes installs instantly, no reset needed.
