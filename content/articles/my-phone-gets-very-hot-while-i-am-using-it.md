The back of the phone becomes hot to touch, especially while gaming, charging, or using the camera. Some warmth is normal, but real heat that burns your hand is a warning. It happens because of heavy apps, using the phone while charging, direct sunlight, a thick cover that blocks heat, or a hidden app working in the background. Constant heat damages the battery and can make the phone slow forever.

## How to fix it

1. Close all apps and let the phone rest for 10 minutes.
2. Do not use the phone while it is charging.
3. Remove the phone cover while charging.
4. Turn off hotspot, GPS and gaming apps when not needed.
5. If it gets hot even when idle, some app is misbehaving. Check battery usage in settings.

## Keep the phone cool
- Close heavy apps running in the background, especially games, maps and the camera - they warm the phone fast.
- Take the case off while charging or gaming. Cases trap heat exactly where the phone needs to lose it.
- Keep the phone out of direct sun - a car dashboard in summer can overheat a phone that is doing nothing.
- Turn off background app refresh and lower screen brightness for a day and watch the difference.
- Charging while gaming is the fastest way to overheat. Avoid it, or at least use a slower charger while playing.
- If the phone gets hot even when idle and the battery drains fast, have the battery checked - a swollen battery is a safety issue, not a settings problem.

Take the case off during heavy use - it is the cheapest cooling upgrade that exists. Cases trap heat exactly where the phone is trying to expel it, and the temperature drop is immediate.
