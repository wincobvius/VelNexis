You need a webcam for meetings, classes or streaming — but your laptop has none, or its camera is terrible, or webcam prices are silly. Your phone's camera is far better than most webcams, and turning it into one is free: built-in features on new phones, or trusted free apps for everyone else.

## How to fix it

1. Easiest: many phones now have a built-in option — Samsung "Phone camera as webcam", Xiaomi and others added similar features.
2. iPhone + Mac: use Continuity Camera (iOS 16 and newer) — automatic and best quality.
3. Free apps like DroidCam or Iriun: install on phone and PC, connect on the same WiFi.
4. In Zoom/Meet, select the phone as the camera source.
5. Put the phone on a stand and plug it into a charger — video drains the battery fast.

## Use your phone as a webcam
- iPhone with a Mac: the phone works as a webcam natively (Continuity Camera) - just hold it near the Mac and pick it in the camera list.
- Android: Camo or DroidCam turn the phone into a webcam over WiFi or USB.
- USB beats WiFi for stability - plug it in and enable USB mode in the app.
- Mount the phone at eye level on a small tripod, in landscape orientation.
- Use the rear camera - it beats almost every laptop webcam.
- Clean the lens and lock the exposure so the picture does not flicker.
- In Zoom or Meet, select 'Camo' or 'DroidCam' as the camera in video settings.
- Bonus: the phone handles the video encoding, so the laptop battery lasts longer in calls.

Lock the phone's exposure before important calls - tap and hold on your face until 'AE/AF lock' appears. Without it, the picture brightens and dims every time you lean back, which reads as flicker to the other side.
