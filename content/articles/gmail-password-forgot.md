You need to log in to Gmail but cannot remember the password — or someone changed it. Your Gmail is the master key to your whole digital life: YouTube, Play Store, Drive, photos, and every account that used "Sign in with Google". Google has a step-by-step recovery process, and its success depends heavily on where and from which device you try.

## How to fix it

1. Go to accounts.google.com/signin/recovery on any device.
2. Type your Gmail address and click "Forgot password".
3. Google will ask to send a code to your recovery phone or email. Use it.
4. If you have no recovery options, answer questions like your last password and when you made the account. Try from a device and place where you used Gmail before — this helps a lot.
5. Set a new password and immediately add a recovery phone and email in settings.

Q: Recovery keeps failing — what am I doing wrong?
A: Two things matter most: try from a device you have used before (home PC, your phone) and answer the questions as accurately as you can. A brand-new device + guessed answers = locked out.

## Quick recap
- Use gmail.com's 'Forgot password' page with a device and browser you used before - Google trusts familiar devices far more.
- Recovery email and phone must be yours and working. Update them today, while you still have access.
- Answer the security questions with old passwords you remember - not the current one.
- Once back in, set a strong new password and turn on 2-step verification.

Write your recovery phone and backup email on paper today, while you are logged in. Recovery works when the details on file are current - and most people discover they are not exactly when they need them most.
