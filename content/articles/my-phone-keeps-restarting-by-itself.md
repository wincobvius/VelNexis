The phone turns off and restarts on its own, again and again, without you touching it. It can happen once a day or every few minutes. The causes are: a bad app that crashes the system, a swollen or weak battery, overheating, or damaged hardware after a drop. If the back cover is lifting or the battery looks raised, stop using the phone — that is dangerous.

## How to fix it

1. Check if it restarts only with one app. If yes, uninstall that app.
2. Remove the phone cover and check if the battery is swollen. A swollen battery is dangerous — replace it fast.
3. Boot in Safe Mode (hold power → long press "Power off" → Safe Mode). If it stops restarting, an app is the problem.
4. Update the phone software.
5. If nothing works, back up data and factory reset.

## Stop the restart loop
- Note when it restarts: during heavy use points to a weak battery; random restarts point to an app or update.
- Boot into Safe Mode (hold power, then long-press Power off). Stable there means a third-party app - uninstall recent ones.
- Check battery health, and look for a lifted screen - a swollen battery restarts phones under load and is a safety risk.
- Storage nearly full also causes restarts. Free space.
- Remove the memory card and test - a corrupt card reboots many phones.
- Started after a system update? Report it and wait for the patch.
- Water or a drop recently? Loose internal connectors need a service center.

Suspect the battery connector if the restarts started after any repair or fall - a slightly loose flex cable produces exactly this symptom, and a technician reseats it in minutes.
