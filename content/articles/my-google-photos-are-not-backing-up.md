You take photos daily, trusting Google Photos to save them to the cloud — then check from another device and find nothing recent. Silent backup failures are the scariest kind because you only discover them when the phone is lost. The causes: backup toggle off, full storage, battery optimization freezing the app, or waiting for WiFi that never comes.

## How to fix it

1. Google Photos app → Profile icon → check "Backup" is ON.
2. Check storage at one.google.com — full storage stops backup.
3. Connect to WiFi and charger — backup prefers those conditions.
4. Open the app and keep it on screen for a while — background backup pauses on some phones due to battery savers.
5. Check battery optimization is OFF for the Photos app in phone settings.

((ad))

## Get backups moving again
- Open Google Photos > profile picture > Photos settings > Backup and check it is on for the right Google account.
- Check the 'Backup over mobile data' toggle - if set to WiFi only, backups pause whenever you leave the house.
- Free up Google storage: photos stop backing up completely when your 15 GB is full. Settings shows exactly what is eating it.
- In Backup settings, open 'Back up device folders' and tick folders like Screenshots or WhatsApp - many are off by default.
- Clear the app cache and force-stop the app once. Backup resumes on restart.
- Leave the phone charging with the screen off overnight once a week - phones pause background backup very aggressively.

## Quick recap
- Check the backup toggle AND the account signed in.
- Storage full stops everything - empty the trash.
- Tick the device folders you want, like WhatsApp.
- Charge overnight with WiFi once a week for the backlog.
