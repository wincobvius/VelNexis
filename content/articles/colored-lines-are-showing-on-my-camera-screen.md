Strange colored lines, static or distortion appear in your camera view — like an old broken TV. Lines that appear only in the camera view (not in saved photos) suggest the display path; lines that burn into saved photos mean the image sensor itself is damaged. The saved-photo test separates the two worlds.

## The separating test

1. **Take a photo of a plain surface** (a wall, a sheet of paper) while the lines are visible, then open that photo in the gallery.
   - **Lines gone in the saved photo** → the sensor is fine; the camera APP or the DISPLAY is at fault. Cheaper world.
   - **Lines burned into the photo** → the image sensor or its connection is damaged. Hardware world.

## Camera-app world (lines not in photos)

2. **Restart the camera app and the phone** — a corrupted preview from a crashed app session draws exactly these lines.
3. **Test another camera app** (Snapchat, WhatsApp camera, or a third-party camera app). Lines there too → display or hardware; lines only in YOUR camera app → clear its cache/data or reinstall it.
4. **Update the phone** — display driver glitches after updates arrive as patches.

## Display world

5. Lines visible in OTHER apps and on the home screen too (even faintly)? Then the camera is innocent — the screen's connection (often after a drop) is failing, and any app shows it eventually. Squeeze-test lightly near the lines: if they dance, it is the ribbon cable. Repair shop.

## Sensor world

6. Lines in saved photos — often after drops, water, or pressure — mean the sensor or its connector. Before the shop: try a full phone restart and, if you dare, a very gentle press near the camera module (a re-seatable connector sometimes wakes). If lines persist, replacement of the camera module is the fix — a routine, moderately priced repair.
