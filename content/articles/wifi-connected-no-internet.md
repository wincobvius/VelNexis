Your phone or laptop shows the WiFi symbol as connected, but websites do not open and apps say "no internet". This is the most common internet problem in every home. It means the device and router are talking to each other, but the internet is not reaching the router — or the device's settings are damaged. The fault can be in the router, the internet company's line, or just one device.

## How to fix it

1. Turn WiFi off on the phone, then turn it on again.
2. Restart the router: unplug power for 30 seconds, then plug it back.
3. Check if other devices have internet. If no device works, the problem is the router or your internet company — call them.
4. If only one device fails, forget the WiFi network and connect again with the password.
5. If websites still do not open, set DNS to 8.8.8.8 in the WiFi advanced settings.

## What the symptom tells you

| Situation | Meaning | Fix |
| One device offline, rest fine | That device's settings or DNS | Restart it, renew the IP, change DNS |
| All devices offline | Router lost the line or its DNS died | Restart the router, check provider status |
| Drops every few minutes | Channel interference or band steering | Move the router, split the 2.4 and 5GHz bands |
| Works near the router, dies far away | Coverage gap, not a fault | Relocate the router or add a repeater halfway |

Q: The router works for everyone else but only my phone says no internet — why?
A: That is almost always the phone's network settings. 'Forget network' and reconnect fixes it most of the time; if not, reset network settings in the phone's Settings app.

## Quick recap
- Test another device on the same WiFi. Working there = your device; dead everywhere = router or provider.
- Change your DNS to 1.1.1.1 or 8.8.8.8 - 'connected, no internet' is very often a DNS failure.
- Restart the router for a full minute, not ten seconds.
- If the router's line light is red, call your provider - nothing on your side fixes that.

One device works and others do not? The router is innocent - the problem is DNS or settings on the failing devices. All devices dead? Restart the router first and test before touching anything else.
