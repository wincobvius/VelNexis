An Android phone that used to fly now hesitates everywhere — keyboard lag, slow app opens, stutters while scrolling. Before the drastic step of a factory reset, work through this order. A slow phone is usually one app or one full storage, not an old phone.

## The diagnostic order

1. **Restart it properly** — hold power and choose restart, not sleep/wake. Phones left on for weeks develop mystery slowness on their own.
2. **Check free storage.** Under roughly 10% free, Android slows down sharply because the system cannot manage its temporary files. Settings → Storage shows the culprit; photos, videos and WhatsApp media are the usual suspects.
3. **Find the app burning CPU.** Settings → Battery → usage since last charge. One app near the top that you barely use is a strong suspect — uninstall or reinstall it.
4. **Trim the heavy stuff:** unused apps (they still wake the phone), third-party launchers, and "cleaner" or "booster" apps — which often cause the slowness they promise to fix.
5. **Reduce animation load:** Developer options → window and transition animation scales → 0.5x. Purely visual, but it makes the phone feel snappier for free.

## If none of that helps

Back up everything important — photos, contacts, chat backups — and do a factory reset as the qualified last resort. If the phone is still slow after a reset, the hardware (often storage wear) is the honest answer.

Q: Do cleaner and booster apps help a slow phone?
A: Mostly the opposite — they run constantly, show ads, and keep the phone awake. The built-in storage and battery screens do the same job for free.
