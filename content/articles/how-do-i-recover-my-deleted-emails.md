You deleted an important email — maybe cleaned the inbox too fast — and now you need it back. Deleted emails are not gone instantly: Gmail keeps them in Trash/Bin for 30 days, like a waiting room before permanent deletion. Inside those 30 days recovery is one click; after that it depends on Gmail's special recovery tool.

## If it is within 30 days

1. **Open Gmail and find Trash (Bin)** in the left folder list — on the app, tap Menu to see all folders.
2. **Search inside Trash** by sender or subject — faster than scrolling when the folder is crowded.
3. **Open the email → "Move to" → Inbox.** It returns instantly, fully readable, with labels intact. You can also select several at once with the checkboxes and move them together.

## If 30 days have passed

4. **Use Gmail's message-recovery tool:** search "Gmail recover deleted messages" and follow Google's official recovery page (it asks you to sign in and submits a restore request). It attempts to restore mail deleted from Trash in a limited window — honest odds: it works sometimes, not always, and works best soon after deletion.
5. **Check where else the email might live:** the sender's copy (ask them to resend — often the fastest fix of all), a forwarding account, a backup, or another device where the mail app had cached it offline.

## Prevention that removes this problem

For mail that matters, archive instead of delete — archived mail leaves the inbox but stays searchable forever, and never expires.
