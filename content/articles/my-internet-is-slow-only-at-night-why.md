All day the internet runs fine, but every evening it becomes slow — videos buffer and pages hang. This pattern has two causes: everyone in the area uses the internet at night (the company's line gets crowded), or someone in your own house starts heavy downloading or streaming in the evening. Watching whether the slowdown matches peak hours tells you which cause it is.

## How to fix it

1. At night everyone uses the internet, so the line gets busy.
2. Check if someone in the house is downloading big files or watching in high quality.
3. Restart the router in the evening to get a fresh connection.
4. If the night speed is always very low compared to your package, complain to your internet company.
5. Ask them to check the line, or upgrade your package.

## Why nights are slow
- Evening congestion is real: everyone in your area streams from 7pm to midnight on the same shared line, so speeds drop even on a perfect connection.
- Test your speed at 8am and again at 9pm. A big gap means congestion - no router setting fixes that, only a plan upgrade or provider change.
- Check your own house too: streaming TVs, downloads and gaming at night all eat the same line. Pause Windows updates during evening hours.
- On WiFi, crowded neighbor channels at night make it worse - switch channels or use a cable.
- Some providers slow down budget packages at peak hours. Ask them directly - they know.
- If night slowness started suddenly, change your WiFi password to rule out a neighbor using your connection.
- Schedule big downloads for early morning - full speed, no waiting.
