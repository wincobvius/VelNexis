You insert a game disc into your PS5 and it spins, whirs — and nothing. The disc never appears on the home screen. The causes climb from harmless (a dirty or scratched disc) to software (a corrupted database) to hardware (a failed drive). One important PS5 fact: its disc drives are paired to the console, so drive swapping is not a user fix.

## How to fix it

1. Clean the disc with a soft cloth from center to edge.
2. Try another disc — one scratched disc proves nothing.
3. Rebuild database: boot in Safe Mode (hold power until second beep) → "Rebuild Database" (games stay safe).
4. Check the disc is inserted with the label facing the correct side.
5. If no disc works, the drive is faulty — contact PlayStation support; drives are paired to the console, so do not swap them yourself.

## Fix the disc drive
- Hold the power button until you hear two beeps (safe mode) > Clear Cache and Rebuild Database.
- Eject the disc fully and reinsert it straight and firm - tilted discs fail to read.
- Clean the disc from center to edge with a soft cloth - fingerprints cause most read failures.
- Update the system software - drive fixes ship inside it.
- Test several discs: some read and some not means scratched discs; none read means the drive.
- Listen for spinning: silence means a loose internal cable (common after transport) or no power to the drive.
- For the Slim model, the drive is replaceable separately - a plug-in fix.

Look at the disc label under a bright light - rental and second-hand discs often carry a thin film that reads as damage. A proper clean fixes more 'broken drives' than any setting.
