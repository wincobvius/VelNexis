You hold your iPhone at the payment terminal and nothing happens — the payment fails, or Apple Pay will not even open. Before assuming the worst, know that the causes range from the trivially local (terminal not reading the phone) to the structural (Apple Pay is simply not available for your bank or country).

## Work through this order

1. **Try the terminal adjustments first.** Hold the top of the phone within 2-3 cm of the contactless symbol, wait for the checkmark, and keep holding until "Done" appears. Ask the cashier to set the terminal to card/contactless payment — some terminals need it switched manually.
2. **Check Face ID / Touch ID is working.** Apple Pay requires it — double-click the side button, authenticate, then hold the phone near. If Face ID fails repeatedly, fall back to your passcode entry at the prompt.
3. **Check the card itself:** open Wallet, tap the card, and look for any "verify" or error banner. A card that failed verification will not pay no matter what the terminal does.
4. **Availability check:** Apple Pay support varies by country and by bank. If your region or bank is not on Apple's supported list, no amount of troubleshooting fixes this — a physical card (or the bank's own contactless app) is the answer.
5. **Still failing everywhere?** Remove the card from Wallet, restart the phone, add the card again fresh. If it declines only at one merchant but works elsewhere, the fault is that terminal or that merchant's payment processor — not your phone.

((ad))

One detail worth checking: cards added through the bank's own app sometimes conflict with Wallet. Remove the card from both, restart the phone, and add it again only through Wallet. Also confirm your region supports Apple Pay with your specific bank - not every issuer in every country is on the list, and the error message rarely says so.
