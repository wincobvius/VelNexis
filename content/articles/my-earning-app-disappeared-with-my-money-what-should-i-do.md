You used an "earning" app — watching ads, investing, referring friends — it paid small amounts at first, then demanded bigger deposits, and one day it vanished: app deleted, website down, numbers blocked. Your money is gone. These are investment scams, and thousands fall for them. There are real steps to take, and real lessons to stop it repeating.

## How to fix it

1. These apps are usually scams. First, stop investing more — that is the biggest protection.
2. Save proof: screenshots of balance, transactions, app name, and the numbers of people who referred you.
3. Report to FIA Cyber Crime at complaint.nr3c.gov.pk or helpline 1991 with the proof.
4. Warn others in the groups where the app was promoted.
5. Real earning never needs you to "add more to unlock withdrawal". Remember this rule.

## If an earning app vanishes with your money
- Search the app name plus 'scam' or 'not paying' first. If others report the same, it was a scam, not bad luck.
- Check the Play Store page - if it says 'not available', the app was removed, often after scam reports.
- Do NOT pay any 'withdrawal fee' or 'tax' to unlock your balance. No real app charges you to take out your own money - that is the second half of the scam.
- Screenshot everything now: balance, referrals, transactions. Evidence matters if recovery is ever possible.
- If you deposited through JazzCash, EasyPaisa or a bank, report the receiver's account to their fraud helpline immediately.
- The hard truth: money lost to scam apps rarely comes back. The real win is reporting fast enough to protect the next person - and never depositing again.
