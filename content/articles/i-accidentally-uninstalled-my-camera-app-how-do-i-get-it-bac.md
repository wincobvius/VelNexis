Your camera app icon vanished — removed by a cleaning app, a curious child, or a mis-tap. Panic is unnecessary: on almost all phones, the camera is a system app that cannot truly be deleted, only disabled. Re-enabling it takes one minute in settings.

## Getting it back

1. **Check the app drawer first** (Android): swipe up on the home screen and search "camera" — more often than not, the app was merely removed from the home screen, not uninstalled. Drag its icon back out.
2. **Re-enable the disabled system app:** Settings → Apps → (show all apps / system apps) → Camera → **Enable**. A disabled camera shows no icon anywhere, which looks exactly like deletion. If you see an "Enable" button, that was the whole problem.
3. **On iPhone:** the Camera app can be hidden via App Library or removed from Home Screen (long-press → Remove App → Remove from Home Screen). Search "Camera" in App Library or Spotlight and drag it back. True deletion is blocked for system apps — and if Screen Time restrictions hide it (Settings → Screen Time → Allowed Apps → Camera toggle), re-enable there.
4. **"App is missing from Apps list too"** (Android): check Settings → Screen Time / Digital Wellbeing restrictions, and any "app lock" or "hide apps" feature in your launcher (Samsung, Xiaomi and others ship these) — hidden apps are invisible everywhere except the hide-list.

## If a third-party cleaner actually deleted it

System apps survive cleaners; but if a non-stock camera was in use and is truly gone, reinstall it from the Play Store/App Store. And then uninstall the cleaner that "helped" — see our guide on why booster/cleaner apps cause more problems than they fix.
