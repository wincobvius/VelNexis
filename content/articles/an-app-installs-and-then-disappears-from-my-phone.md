You install an app, it works — and days later it is simply gone from the phone, as if deleted by a ghost. This has a real explanation: either Play Store removed it (not available in your country), or Play Protect silently uninstalled it as a harmful app. Play Protect keeps a history showing exactly what it removed and why.

## How to fix it

1. This happens with apps not available in your country — Play Store removes them.
2. It also happens with fake or harmful apps — Play Protect removes them automatically.
3. Check Play Protect history: Play Store → Profile → Play Protect — it shows removed apps and reasons.
4. If it was a needed app, install it only from the official site of that service.
5. If it keeps happening with normal apps, scan the phone for malware.

## Stop apps from vanishing
- iPhone: check the App Library (swipe left past the last home screen) and hidden home screens (long-press the screen, tap the dots) - apps hide more often than they delete.
- Android: check Settings > Apps - if the app is listed but invisible, it is disabled. Tap it and press Enable.
- Some phones auto-remove 'unused' apps: turn off 'uninstall unused apps' in Play Store settings and any storage manager feature.
- Kid mode and work profiles hide apps - check if a profile is active.
- Launcher glitch: restart the phone or switch launchers - icons reappear.
- Truly gone? Reinstall from the store - app data is usually safe in your cloud account.
- If it vanishes again on a schedule, a cleaner or 'game booster' app is deleting it - disable that automation.
