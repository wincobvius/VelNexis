You have one printer and several computers, and you want them all printing without buying anything. Two architectures exist: connect the printer itself to WiFi (best — no computer dependency), or share a USB printer through one PC (works, but that PC must stay on). Each path has its own setup steps.

## How to fix it

1. Best way: connect the printer itself to WiFi — then every computer adds it directly.
2. If the printer is USB-only: connect it to one PC, Control Panel → Devices and Printers → right click printer → Printer properties → Sharing tab → Share this printer.
3. On other computers, add a network printer and choose the shared one.
4. The sharing computer must be ON for others to print.
5. Network printers are easier — sharing through a PC creates dependency.

## Share one printer with all home PCs
- WiFi printers need no sharing at all: just add the printer on each PC once (Settings > Printers > Add) and every PC prints directly.
- Older USB printer: on the PC where it is plugged in, go to Printer properties > Sharing > Share this printer.\- All PCs must be on the same WiFi with network discovery turned on.
- The host PC must stay on for others to print - that is the main limit of USB sharing.
- On other PCs: Add printer > 'Select a shared printer by name' and enter the host PC's name plus the printer share name.
- A cheap print server box, or upgrading to a WiFi printer, removes the host-PC-on requirement forever.
- Heavy printing from many PCs queues on the host - expect some waiting at homework time.
