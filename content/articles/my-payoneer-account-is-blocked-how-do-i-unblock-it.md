Your Payoneer account — the bridge that brings your freelance dollars to Pakistan — suddenly shows blocked, limited or "under review". Your earnings may be sitting inside. Blocks happen when the system spots two accounts on one person, unclear documents, logins from many devices, or transactions that look odd. All are usually reversible through Payoneer's review process.

## The unblock path

1. **Read the exact message and email** — Payoneer states the reason category: "additional verification required", "documents needed", or a policy review. Your next move depends on which one it is.
2. **Check the Verification Center:** log in (even a limited account usually opens this page) — it lists the exact pending items: ID document, proof of address, bank statement, or source-of-funds explanation. Upload clear, valid, matching documents — full document, all corners, name identical to the account. Fuzzy or mismatched uploads are the #1 reason reviews drag on.
3. **Source of funds questions:** earnings from Fiverr/Upwork? A screenshot of the payout page or the platform's earnings statement answers it. Payoneer must satisfy regulators that the money is legitimate freelance income — the platform statements prove it in one upload.
4. **The two-account trap:** if you ever opened an older Payoneer account (even years ago, even unused), that duplication alone triggers blocks. Tell support which account to keep; they merge/close and release the active one. Do not open a NEW account while one is under review — it deepens the problem badly.
5. **Contact support properly:** live chat from inside the account, or the support center ticket — include your customer ID and the exact blocker message. Response time runs days; one clear ticket beats five angry ones.

## Prevention

Logins from many devices/locations (VPN on, then off) look like account sharing — keep VPN use consistent or off for Payoneer. And withdraw regularly rather than parking large balances: smaller balances mean smaller stakes if a review ever hits.
