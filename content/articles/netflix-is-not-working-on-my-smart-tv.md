Netflix refuses to work on your TV — the app will not open, shows an error code, spins forever, or logs you out repeatedly. The Netflix app lives inside the TV like a small computer program, and it fails from the same causes as phone apps: bad cache, old versions, account problems, or the TV's own clock being wrong (a surprisingly common culprit).

## How to fix it

1. Check other apps — if all fail, it is your internet, not Netflix.
2. Restart the TV (unplug for 1 minute).
3. Update the Netflix app, or delete and reinstall it.
4. Check your Netflix account is active — log in on your phone to test.
5. Check the date and time on the TV — wrong time breaks app security.

## Fix Netflix on the TV
- Fully restart the TV: unplug it from power for one full minute, then plug it back in. A normal standby restart does not clear the app memory.
- Sign out of Netflix inside the app and sign back in - this clears broken session data.
- Test your internet speed on the TV or check another app like YouTube. Netflix needs about 5 Mbps for HD - slow lines cause most errors.
- Reinstall the Netflix app from the TV's app store.
- If the picture is black but the sound plays, change the HDMI port or cable, and turn off extra HDMI features for that input.
- Note the exact error code on screen - it tells you if the problem is the network or the app.

Check another streaming app first - if YouTube also fails, Netflix is innocent and the TV's connection is the patient. Isolating the app from the network saves an hour of wrong fixes.
