Your phone shows "storage full" and refuses to save photos, download apps or update software. This happens because videos, photos, WhatsApp files and app data slowly fill the storage over months. When storage is full, the phone also becomes slow and apps start crashing. Fixing it is free and takes only a few minutes.

## How to fix it

1. Open Settings → Storage. See what is taking space.
2. Delete old videos and photos you do not need.
3. Clear app cache: Settings → Storage → Cached data → Clear.
4. Uninstall apps you never open.
5. Move photos to Google Photos and delete them from the phone.

Q: What is eating my storage that I can never find?
A: Usually WhatsApp — its media folder grows for years silently. WhatsApp → Settings → Storage and data shows the exact culprits per chat.

## Free space properly
- Settings > Storage lists the biggest items first - start at the top, not by guessing.
- Chat media is the hidden giant: WhatsApp > Settings > Storage shows every category. Years of saved videos live there.
- Move photos to Google Photos and run its 'Free up space' tool - photos stay in the cloud, the phone gets empty space.
- Empty the Downloads folder - old PDFs and installers pile up for years.
- Delete heavy games you finished - progress is usually saved in your account.
- Clear app caches for the top few apps (not their data).
- If the phone takes a microSD card, move photos and music to it.
- Skip 'cleaner' apps - they show big numbers, free little, and run ads all day.

Sort WhatsApp media by size inside the app (Storage settings) rather than deleting photos manually - the biggest space thieves are almost always video categories you forgot existed.
