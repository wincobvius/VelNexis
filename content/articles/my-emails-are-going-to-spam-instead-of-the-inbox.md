Emails from a specific person, company or service keep landing in your spam folder even though they are genuine. You miss important mails because everyone checks spam rarely. This happens when too many people marked that sender as spam, or when your own filters or past actions taught your inbox to distrust that sender. You can retrain it.

## How to fix it

1. Open the spam folder, select the email, click "Not spam".
2. Add the sender to contacts: open the email → three dots → "Add to contacts".
3. Create a filter: search the sender's name → three dots → "Filter messages" → "Never send to spam".
4. Ask the sender to send from a proper business email, not a strange address.
5. If it is important mail like bank or office mail, also mark earlier spam mails from them as not spam.

## Land in the inbox, not spam
- Ask the receiver to drag your mail from Spam to Inbox and add you as a contact - the strongest signal for their provider.
- Free addresses full of numbers, ALL-CAPS subjects and too many links trip filters. Write like a human.
- Avoid spam habits: words like FREE and URGENT, many exclamation marks, huge attachments, and 20 people in the To field.
- For business mail, ask your hosting provider to set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC records - a 10-minute fix that changes everything.
- Plain text reaches better than heavy image-only templates.
- New addresses should start slow - a fresh account mailing 50 people a day looks exactly like spam.
- If your mails to one company keep vanishing, ask them to have IT whitelist your address.
