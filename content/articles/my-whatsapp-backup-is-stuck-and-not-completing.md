You try to back up your chats and the progress bar freezes at some percent — sometimes 0%, sometimes 90%. Without a backup, a lost or broken phone means losing all chats forever. Backups fail when Google Drive is full, the phone is not on WiFi, the Google account is not connected, or the backup file has grown very big with years of videos.

## How to fix it

1. Check Google Drive has free space (free accounts get 15 GB).
2. Check your Google account is signed in: WhatsApp → Settings → Chats → Chat backup → Google account.
3. Use WiFi for backup. Big backups fail on mobile data.
4. Keep the phone charging and the screen on during backup.
5. If it fails again, delete the old WhatsApp backup from Google Drive and start a new backup.

## Get the backup moving
- Backup needs solid WiFi AND enough battery - plug in the charger and retry.
- Check storage on both ends: a full Google Drive or a full phone stops the backup silently.
- Clear WhatsApp's cache (Settings > Apps > WhatsApp > Storage) - do not clear data.
- In WhatsApp backup settings, turn off 'Include videos' - big videos are the usual reason backups hang halfway for hours.
- Sign out of Google Drive inside WhatsApp backup settings and sign back in.
- On iPhone, check iCloud storage - the free 5 GB fills fast, and media may need excluding.
- Last resort: reinstall WhatsApp (your chats stay on the phone), then run the backup before doing anything else.

Run the backup on charge, on WiFi, with the screen off, overnight. Background limits pause mid-backup constantly - the same backup that stuck for days completes in one quiet hour.
