Your iPhone fell in water — sink, toilet, pool, rain — and every second counts. What people do in the first minutes often decides whether the phone lives. Modern iPhones have some water resistance, but it is not waterproof, and water damage appears gradually over days. There are right steps and famous wrong steps (rice!).

## How to fix it

1. Take it out fast. Do not press buttons and do not charge it.
2. Dry the outside with a cloth. Do not use a hair dryer or put it in rice — rice does not really help.
3. Keep it upright in a dry, airy place for several hours.
4. Modern iPhones show a liquid warning in the port — wait until it is fully dry before charging.
5. Even if it works, water damage appears later. Back up data immediately and visit service if behavior changes.

## The right steps, in order
- Take it out and power it OFF - not lock, off.
- Remove the case and SIM tray and shake the water out of the ports gently.
- Leave it in moving air for 24 to 48 hours, port-side down. Skip the rice - it only adds dust.
- Do NOT charge it, blow hot air on it, or keep pressing buttons - each pushes water deeper.
- Seawater or pool water needs a shop cleaning within a day or two - salt corrodes boards fast.
- Newer iPhones resist water, but that seal weakens with every drop and year.
- If it revives, back up everything the same day - water damage returns without warning weeks later.

Do not test the speakers right after drying - playing sound pushes water deeper. Modern iPhones eject water themselves if you play a low-frequency tone or simply wait; patience beats poking at ports.
