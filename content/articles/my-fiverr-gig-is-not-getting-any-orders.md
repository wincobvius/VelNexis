Your service listing (gig) is live on Fiverr, days or weeks have passed, and the order counter stays at zero. You are not alone — thousands of new gigs die quietly every day. The cause is rarely "no demand": it is usually a gig that does not match what buyers search, weak presentation, no video, or pricing that gives no reason to trust a new seller.

## How to fix it

1. Check your gig title matches what buyers search ("I will design a modern logo for your business").
2. Use all 5 search tags buyers type.
3. Add a gig video — gigs with videos get many more orders.
4. Price low in the start and collect 5-star reviews first, then raise the price.
5. Share your gig on social media to bring the first visitors.

## Get the first orders
- Check your gig is actually visible: open Fiverr in a logged-out browser and search your gig keywords. If it is nowhere in the first pages, visibility is the problem, not buyers.
- Redo the first image: big clear text with your offer, and your face if you sell services. Buyers click the image before reading anything.
- Compare your gig honestly with the top 5 in your category: fewer samples, higher price, slower delivery? Fix the biggest gap.
- Start with a lower price and fast delivery - the first 5 reviews matter more than profit.
- Send offers daily in Buyer Requests - early orders come from offers, not search.
- Reply within an hour using the Fiverr app - response time is visible to buyers.
- No orders after weeks? Publish 3 to 5 related gigs. Each gig is another fishing hook.
