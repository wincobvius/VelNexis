You connect your iPhone to the computer to transfer photos or back up — and the computer shows nothing, as if no phone is plugged in. The causes are usually small: a charge-only cable, the "Trust This Computer" prompt ignored, an old iTunes version, or Windows driver issues. The cable is guilty in most houses.

## The checks, in the order that finds it fastest

1. **Try another cable — and make it a data cable.** Countless "dead connection" cases are charge-only USB cables: they power the phone but carry no data. A cable that came with another phone (or an original Apple one) settles the question in seconds.
2. **Watch the phone when you plug in.** The "Trust This Computer?" prompt appears on the IPHONE, not the computer — tap Trust, enter your passcode. If you missed it, unplug and replug. No prompt and no charging icon at all → cable or port.
3. **Unlock the phone before connecting.** A locked iPhone stays invisible to the computer by design; unlock it, then replug.
4. **Try another USB port** (prefer a port on the machine itself, not a hub or monitor), and another computer if available — isolation finds the guilty end fast.
5. **On Windows:** install the current iTunes or the Apple Devices app from the Microsoft Store — they bring the drivers that make Windows see iPhones. An old or never-installed iTunes is the classic Windows cause.
6. **Clean the phone's charging port** — pocket lint builds up until cables sit loose. A dry, soft brush (never a pin) clears it.

## If photos still do not appear

Once the iPhone IS detected, Windows imports via File Explorer (Apple iPhone → Internal Storage) — but only photos in the camera roll. Music, apps and full backups go through iTunes/the Apple Devices app.
