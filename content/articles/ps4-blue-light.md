You press power on your PS4, the light turns blue and blinks — and the TV never shows anything. This is the famous "Blue Light of Death": the console starts but fails to complete its startup. Causes range from a power glitch (fixed by a full power drain) to HDMI faults to a failing power supply.

## How to fix it

1. Unplug the power cable from the wall and the console.
2. Wait 2–3 minutes — this clears the power board.
3. Hold the power button for 7 seconds while plugging the cable back; the console beeps twice.
4. Try another wall socket.
5. The blue light can also mean an HDMI handshake issue — try another HDMI port and cable. If nothing works, it needs repair (likely the power supply).

((ad))

Q: Is the blue light of death always serious?
A: No — a large share of cases are just power glitches fixed by the full drain in steps 1–3. Only when that fails repeatedly does it point to hardware.

## Work the blue light
- Full power drain first: unplug 60 seconds, hold the power button 30 seconds, replug.
- Try another HDMI cable and TV port - HDMI failure copies blue-light death exactly.
- Boot to safe mode (hold power until the second beep) > Change Resolution - a bad display handshake sticks the boot.
- Safe mode > Rebuild Database fixes corrupted system data.
- Restore Default Settings keeps your games. Initialize is the last resort - the USB method with the official firmware file.
- A failing hard drive also causes the blue light - any laptop drive or SSD works as a replacement.
- Safe mode never loading at all means hardware - the HDMI chip or power supply. Service territory.
