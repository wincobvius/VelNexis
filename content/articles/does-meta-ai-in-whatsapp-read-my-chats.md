A blue-purple circle appeared in WhatsApp — Meta AI — and the worry followed naturally: is this assistant quietly reading my conversations with friends and family?

## What it can and cannot see

According to how WhatsApp presents the feature, Meta AI takes in only the messages where you involve it: tagging @Meta AI in a chat, or opening a direct conversation with the assistant. Your ordinary chats with people are end-to-end encrypted and are not scanned to feed the assistant. Those are two separate lanes.

## If you do not want it

You cannot fully remove the button in most regions, but you are not obliged to use it. Ignore the circle and it stays out of your conversations.

## What to keep in mind

- Treat chats with Meta AI like any AI service: fine for general questions, not the place for passwords, ID numbers or private financial details.
- Policies and regional availability change. For today's exact wording, check WhatsApp's privacy policy and the Meta AI notice inside the app.
- Our related article "Can AI read my WhatsApp chats?" covers the encryption side of the same worry from a different angle.

Q: Did Meta AI read my old chats before I ever used it?
A: There is no indication of that. Person-to-person chats are end-to-end encrypted, and the assistant is built around messages you deliberately send it.

## Quick recap
- Meta AI is a separate optional chat - it only processes what you send to it there.
- Your normal WhatsApp chats stay end-to-end encrypted.
- The Meta AI button can be disabled if you keep tapping it by accident.
- Never forward private chats to any AI 'to summarize' - that copy leaves encryption.
