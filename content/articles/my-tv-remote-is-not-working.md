You press buttons on the TV remote and nothing happens — no response from the TV at all, or only some buttons work. Before blaming the remote, know that 90% of "broken" remotes just need batteries, and there is a magical phone-camera trick that shows whether a remote is actually transmitting.

## How to fix it

1. Change the batteries first — 90% of remote problems are batteries.
2. Check nothing blocks the TV's IR receiver.
3. Test the remote with your phone camera: point the remote at the camera, press buttons — you should see a light flash on the screen.
4. If no light shows, the remote is dead — buy the same model or a universal remote.
5. For smart remotes, re-pair them with the TV (check your TV brand's pairing steps).

## Fix the remote first
- New batteries first - and check the contacts for rust. Wipe them clean.
- Test the remote with your phone camera: point it at the camera and press buttons. If you see a light flash on the phone screen, the remote is sending. No flash means the remote is dead.
- Clean inside: open the remote and wipe the button pad with a slightly damp cloth - worn pads are the top cause of dead buttons.
- If the TV responds only from very close, the infrared LED is weak - replacement remotes are cheap and matched by TV model.
- Smart remotes use Bluetooth: re-pair them by holding the buttons shown on the TV's pairing screen.
- Meanwhile, the TV maker's phone app controls the TV over WiFi.
- Test with the app: if the app works but remotes do not, the TV's sensor needs service, not the remote.
