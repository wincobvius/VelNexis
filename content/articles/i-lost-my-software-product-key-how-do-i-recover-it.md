You need to reinstall a paid program (Office, Windows, a design tool) but the product key is lost — the email is gone, the box was thrown away, the sticker faded. The key is not really lost: it lives in purchase emails, in your account on the maker's site, or inside Windows itself where a command can read it.

## How to fix it

1. Check your email for the purchase receipt — keys are usually there.
2. Check your account on the software maker's website — purchases are saved there.
3. For Windows: Command Prompt as admin, type wmic path softwarelicensingservice get OA3xOriginalProductKey and press Enter.
4. If it came with a box, check the sticker inside the box or on the DVD cover.
5. Contact the seller's support with your purchase proof — they can resend the key.

## Find lost product keys
- Search your email for the maker's name plus 'order' or 'license' - digital purchases live in the inbox.
- For Windows on brand PCs, run this in Command Prompt: wmic path softwarelicensingservice get OA3xOriginalProductKey - it shows the key stored in the BIOS.
- For Office and other software, free tools like ShowKeyPlus or ProduKey read installed keys from the system.
- If Windows came with the PC, the key sticker may be on the bottom of a laptop or the back of a desktop.\-- Sign in at [account.microsoft.com/services](https://account.microsoft.com/services) - licenses tied to your Microsoft account are listed there and reactivate without any key.
- Photograph any sticker keys today - stickers rub off with age.
- If nothing turns up, the honest answer is a new license. Very cheap 'OEM key' websites are gray market - they work until they suddenly do not.
