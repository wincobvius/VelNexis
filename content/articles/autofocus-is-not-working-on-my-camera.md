Your camera hunts for focus endlessly — blurring, sharpening, blurring again — or simply refuses to focus on anything. Autofocus depends on a tiny motor in the lens; when it fails (often after drops) or gets confused (dirty lens, macro mode stuck), photos come out soft. Tapping to force focus is both a fix and a test.

## The quick tests first

1. **Clean the lens** — a fingerprint smudge scatters the light autofocus reads, and "focus problems" die with a microfiber wipe surprisingly often.
2. **Tap-to-focus as a diagnostic:** tap a clear, well-lit object 30-50 cm away. The box appears, the lens visibly adjusts (watch the image snap). Works → autofocus lives; the earlier failures were mode or scene problems. No reaction at all → deeper trouble, keep reading.
3. **Check the mode:** macro mode stuck on, a "pro/manual" mode with focus set to manual, or a 2x/tele lens selected too close to the subject — all produce hunting that looks like failure. Switch to plain Auto/Photo mode and retest.

## The fixes

4. **Restart the camera app fully** (close from recent apps) and then the phone. A camera app's memory stuck in a bad state is the most common software cause.
5. **Clear the camera app's cache/data** (Android: Settings → Apps → Camera → Storage). On iPhone, offload/reinstall the app. This resets saved mode states that no restart reaches.
6. **Update the phone's software** — focus bugs are frequently fixed in camera firmware updates shipped with OS updates.

## If it is hardware

The test: hold the phone to your ear near the camera and open the camera. A healthy autofocus motor makes tiny ticking sounds as it hunts. Silence plus permanent soft focus — especially after a drop — means the focus motor or its connector failed. That is a repair-shop job; no app reinstall fixes a motor.
