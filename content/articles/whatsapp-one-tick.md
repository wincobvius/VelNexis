You send a message and it shows one grey tick, which means it left your phone but did not reach the other person. Two ticks mean delivered; blue ticks mean seen. One tick can mean their phone is off, they have no signal, they blocked you, or your own internet is weak. Most of the time it is harmless — their phone is just off.

## How to fix it

1. One tick means the message left your phone but did not reach them.
2. The other person's phone may be off or out of network — wait.
3. Check your own internet. Open a website to test.
4. Ask the other person to check their internet and restart their phone.
5. If it stays one tick for many days, they may have deleted WhatsApp or blocked you.

((ad))

Q: Does one tick mean I am blocked?
A: Not by itself — being blocked also shows one tick, but so does a switched-off phone. A missing profile photo plus no status plus one tick for days is the stronger signal.

## What one tick really means
- One tick means the message reached WhatsApp's server - not the other phone. It is not automatically a block.
- The phone is probably offline: dead battery, no internet, airplane mode. Most one-ticks resolve within hours.
- Check their last seen and profile photo: no change for days points to a dead phone or deleted account.
- Try a voice call: ringing normally while messages sit at one tick means chats are muted, not blocked.
- Photo gone and calls never connecting looks like a block - WhatsApp never confirms it.
- Check your own internet first - sometimes your connection stalls the upload.
- If everyone's messages are stuck, check a live outage map - sometimes it is WhatsApp itself.
