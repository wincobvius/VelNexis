Every month, money quietly leaves your card for subscriptions — some you use, some you forgot, some you thought you cancelled. Auto-renewing subscriptions are designed to be forgotten. The detail most people miss is that cancelling properly means checking **two places**: the service's own settings, and your phone's app store if you originally subscribed through it.

## The cancellation routine

1. **Identify the charge first.** Search your statement for the exact merchant name — it is often a billing company's name, not the service's. That name is what you search for in the steps below.
2. **Cancel at the source:** log into the service's website → Account or Settings → Subscription (sometimes Billing or Payments) → Cancel. Screenshot the confirmation — it is your evidence if a charge appears anyway.
3. **Subscribed through Google Play?** Play Store → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → the subscription → Cancel. Deleting the app does NOT cancel an app-store subscription — a classic and expensive misunderstanding.
4. **Subscribed through the App Store?** Settings → your name → Subscriptions → the subscription → Cancel. Same rule: deleting the app changes nothing.
5. **Still charged after cancelling?** With your confirmation screenshot in hand, dispute the charge with your bank — and report the merchant's page to the card network if it happens repeatedly.

## One prevention habit

Turn on payment notifications for your card (in your banking app). Forgotten subscriptions survive on silence; a notification on every charge ends them.
