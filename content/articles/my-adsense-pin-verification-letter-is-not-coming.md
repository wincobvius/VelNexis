Your AdSense account is earning, but it is on hold until you verify your address with a PIN code that Google mails on a paper letter. Weeks pass and the letter never arrives — a common problem in many countries where postal service is unreliable. If the PIN is not entered within 4 months, ads stop. There are proper ways out of this trap.

## How to fix it

1. AdSense allows 3 PIN attempts and gives 4 months.
2. Check your address in AdSense settings exactly matches your house address.
3. Letters often arrive late — wait the full 4 weeks.
4. Ask your local post office with the tracking info if available.
5. After 3 failed attempts, AdSense shows an option to verify with an ID document — use that.

## What to do about the PIN
- PINs can take up to 4 weeks to arrive by post, and post in Pakistan is often slower. Count from the issue date shown in your AdSense account.
- You can request a new PIN twice (3 PINs in total) - the button unlocks about 4 weeks after the last request.
- After the third PIN fails to arrive, AdSense usually opens a video ID verification option instead. Check the Payments > Verification page.
- Make sure your payee address is complete - a missing house number or wrong area means the postman cannot deliver.
- Your earnings keep growing in the account while you wait. Nothing is lost.
- Never enter your PIN through any email link - Google never asks for it by email. Those are fake mails.

Request the PIN the day the button unlocks - many people wait weeks between allowed requests, and those weeks add directly to the payment delay. The clock only starts when you act.
