How do I connect my printer to WiFi step by step

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 5, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 5, 2026

You want your printer on the WiFi so every computer and phone in the house can print without cables. Printer WiFi setup sounds like an engineer's task, but every modern printer has a guided wizard that does the hard part. The one thing you need ready: your WiFi password, typed carefully on that difficult printer screen.

Before you start

Put the printer and the router in the same room for setup (move it back later). Weak signal during pairing is the #1 cause of "setup failed" loops.

The three routes

  1. WPS — the button shortcut (if your router has a WPS button): on the printer's WiFi menu, choose WPS / Push Button, then press the router's WPS button within 2 minutes. They find each other and pair — no password typing at all. The fastest route when available.
  2. The printer's setup wizard: WiFi/Network Settings (on the printer's screen or its buttons) → WiFi Setup Wizard → choose YOUR network from the list → type the password carefully (use the printer's arrow-key keyboard patiently; wrong password = "connection failed" that looks like a broken printer). Confirm, and the WiFi icon on the printer settles solid.
  3. The maker's app does it for you: HP Smart, Canon PRINT, Epson iPrint, Brother Mobile Connect — install on your phone, choose "add a new printer", and the app walks the whole setup (some even transfer the phone's WiFi settings to the printer automatically). The friendliest route for most people today.

Verify it worked

Print the printer's own network configuration page (its menu has the option) — it shows the connected network name and signal strength. And from a phone on the same WiFi: try printing anything — success there means the printer side is done forever; computer problems after this are computer-side (see our "computer cannot find printer" guide).
