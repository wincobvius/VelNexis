Apps keep crashing on my phone again and again

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 27, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 27, 2026

One "App has stopped" is annoying. But when app after app closes on its own, the problem is no longer any single app — the phone itself is struggling. The usual culprits: storage that is nearly full, a bad system update, or an app that is dragging everything down with it.

Work through this order

  1. Check free storage first. Below roughly 10% free, Android cannot manage its temporary files and apps crash constantly. Settings → Storage shows the culprit — photos, videos and WhatsApp media are the usual suspects.
  2. Restart properly — hold power and choose Restart, not sleep/wake. Phones left running for weeks collect exactly this kind of instability.
  3. Find the starting point. Did the crashes begin right after a system update? Check for a follow-up patch — makers ship fixes quickly when an update misbehaves. Did they begin after installing one particular app? Uninstall that app and watch what happens.
  4. Update everything pending in the Play Store. Old app versions running on a new system version are a classic mass-crash cause.
  5. Clear data only for the worst offender (Settings → Apps → the app → Storage → Clear data). You will need to sign into that app again — everything else stays.

If apps still crash everywhere

Back up photos, contacts and chat backups, then do a factory reset as the qualified last resort — it erases the phone completely. If apps still crash on a freshly reset phone, the storage chip itself is failing, and that is a repair-shop conversation.

Why does a full storage crash apps?

Apps constantly write small temporary files. When there is no room, those writes fail — and the app shuts down instead of continuing half-broken.
