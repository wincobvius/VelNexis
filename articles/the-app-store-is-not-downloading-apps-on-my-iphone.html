The App Store is not downloading apps on my iPhone

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 23, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 23, 2026

You tap "Get" in the App Store, the icon spins — and nothing installs, or it says "could not be downloaded". Downloads fail from weak internet, Apple ID problems, payment method errors, or restrictions set in Screen Time. Most people never suspect the Screen Time one, yet it blocks downloads silently.

The ladder

  1. Internet check — try a webpage; then toggle Wi-Fi off and download over mobile data (or vice versa). App Store downloads over weak Wi-Fi fail with spins that look like account problems.
  2. Sign out and back into the App Store: App Store → your profile icon (top right) → scroll down → Sign Out → sign back in. This resets the store session — the fastest cure for spins and "cannot connect" errors.
  3. Check your Apple ID payment method: App Store → profile → payment method. An expired or declined card can block ALL downloads, free ones included. Clearing the red-flagged card (or adding a valid one) unblocks the queue.
  4. The Screen Time wall: Settings → Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases — if "Installing Apps" is set to "Don't Allow", the App Store installs nothing, silently, with no error explaining why. This is the one parents set and forget, and repair shops see it weekly.
  5. Restart the phone — clears stuck download states — then retry the download from the App Store's update tab.

The stubborn cases

  1. Nothing installs even after all this: Settings → General → Date & Time — set to automatic; a wrong clock breaks the store's secure handshake. And check Apple's System Status page for App Store outages before blaming your phone — when the whole world cannot download, the fix is patience.
