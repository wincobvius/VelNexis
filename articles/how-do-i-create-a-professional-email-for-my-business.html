How do I create a professional email for my business

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 23, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 23, 2026

You are running a business but your email is something like yourshop123@gmail.com — and customers trust it less. A professional email like info@yourshop.com makes a small business look real and reliable. It needs two things: your own domain name (yourshop.com) and an email service connected to it. The cost is small; the trust gain is big.

The two steps

  1. Buy your domain name — yourshop.com or yourbusiness.pk — from any domain registrar. This is your permanent address on the internet; even if you change email providers later, the address stays yours. Pick the closest match to your business name that is available.
  2. Connect an email service to it. The main options:
  • Google Workspace — Gmail with your own domain (info@yourshop.com), same interface you already know, works with Drive and Meet. Paid per user per month.
  • Microsoft 365 — Outlook with your domain, the standard choice if you already use Word and Excel heavily.
  • Zoho Mail — the budget option with a genuine free tier for a few addresses, good enough for very small businesses.
  1. Create the addresses you actually need: info@ (general), yourname@ (you personally), and maybe orders@ or support@. More than three is usually administration without benefit.
  2. Follow the provider's domain-verification steps (they give you a few codes to paste at your domain registrar). It looks technical but is copy-paste; support walks you through it if stuck.

Worth knowing

Set up the addresses before printing cards or sharing them publicly, and keep the domain renewal on auto-pay — an expired domain releases your address to anyone.

Two small choices that matter: pick name@ over info@ if you are a one-person business (people reply more to a human), and buy the domain before printing anything - cards, packaging, listings - so the address never has to change.
