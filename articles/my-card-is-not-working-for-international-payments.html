My card is not working for international payments

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 31, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 31, 2026

Your card works at every local shop but fails on foreign websites — Netflix, a hosting renewal, a US store. This is usually deliberate: many banks, especially in Pakistan and similar markets, keep international transactions switched off by default as a fraud protection. Once you know the exact words to ask your bank for, weeks of failed payments end in one call.

The fix, step by step

  1. Enable international transactions. Your banking app may have the switch yourself (look for "Card controls" or "Transaction limits" under the card settings — often split into "e-commerce", "POS" and "ATM" categories; international online payments need the e-commerce one ON). Otherwise, call the helpline and say exactly: "Please enable international e-commerce transactions on my card."
  2. Check the daily limit. International transactions often have a separate, lower daily cap than local ones. If your payment exceeds it, ask for a temporary or permanent increase.
  3. Check the currency situation. Your card will be billed in the foreign currency and converted by your bank's rate, usually with a conversion margin — ask what it is, so a $10 charge doesn't surprise you on the statement. Some sites offer to charge you in rupees instead (dynamic currency conversion) — declining that and paying in the site's own currency is usually cheaper.
  4. Try the payment again after enabling — and if it still declines, ask the bank why the specific transaction failed: their system shows the exact decline reason (limit, currency, merchant category), which beats guessing.
  5. If your bank simply won't enable it, alternatives exist: Wise and Payoneer issue cards built for international online payments, and many Pakistanis use them for exactly this reason.

Worth knowing

Some banks also block specific merchant categories or countries by default. Asking "is this merchant or country blocked on my card?" during the same call saves a second call later.
