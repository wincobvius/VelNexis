My browser keeps asking to save passwords again and again

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Sep 2, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Sep 2, 2026

For the same websites, the browser asks "Save password?" every single time — even though you said yes before, or you told it never to ask. This loop happens when the saved password is old (you changed it on the site but not in the browser), when sync is off, or when a cookie resets the site's memory. Updating the saved password ends the nagging.

How to fix it

  1. Chrome settings → Autofill → Passwords → check "Offer to save" is ON and working.
  2. Delete the old wrong saved password for that site — the browser keeps re-asking when the saved one is outdated.
  3. Sign in to Chrome with your Google account so passwords sync and stop repeating.
  4. Clear the site's cookies once.
  5. Update the browser.

Stop the repeat questions

  • Sign out of the browser and sign back in. A broken sync session keeps forgetting what it saved.
  • Check that sync is on for passwords specifically - sync can be on for bookmarks and off for passwords.
  • Clear the browser's cookies (not the passwords) and restart it.
  • If you clicked 'Never save' for a site by mistake: open password settings and remove that site from the 'Never save' list.
  • Turn the 'Offer to save passwords' setting off and on once.
  • Create a new browser profile and sign in - everything syncs back clean. A two-minute fix when nothing else works.

Quick recap

  • Sign out of the browser and back in - a broken sync session causes most repeat prompts.
  • Sync must be on for passwords specifically - bookmark sync is a separate switch.
  • Remove the site from the 'Never save' list if you clicked it by mistake.
  • A fresh profile that syncs back clean beats every setting tweak.
