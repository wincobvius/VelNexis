My browser keeps redirecting me to random websites

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 25, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 25, 2026

You click one link and land on a totally different page — usually ads, gambling or fake "your system is infected" pages. This is browser hijacking: something has taken control of where your clicks lead. The causes are bad extensions, a changed search engine, a modified shortcut, or adware on the PC. Each one has a specific removal step.

Remove the hijack, layer by layer

  1. Extensions first: menu → Extensions → Manage extensions. Remove everything you do not recognize — especially anything installed "around the same time" this started. Hijackers hide as innocuous names like "PDF Helper" or "Search Protect".
  2. Reset the search engine: Settings → Search engine → Manage — set it back to Google (or your real choice). Hijackers swap the search engine so every search routes through their page first — the most common redirect of all.
  3. Check the shortcut trick (classic on Windows): right-click your browser shortcut → Properties → Target field. It should end in `chrome.exe"` and NOTHING more. Hijackers append their URL after it, so opening Chrome opens their page. Delete any trailing text, click OK.
  4. On the homepage/startup: Settings → On startup — remove hijack pages from "Open a specific page", set your real homepage.
  5. Still redirecting? Adware on the PC itself: run a full Windows Security scan plus a Malwarebytes second scan, and uninstall unknown programs from Settings → Apps (sort by install date).

After cleanup

The fake "your system is infected" pages sometimes install a fear first: if you called a number or installed their "helpful tool" from such a page, remove that tool too and scan — it IS the infection.
