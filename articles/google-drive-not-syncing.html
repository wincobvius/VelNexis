Google Drive Is Not Syncing My Files

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Sep 10, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Sep 10, 2026

You save a file on one device, but it never appears on your other devices or in the cloud — Google Drive's sync silently stopped. Sync is Drive's whole purpose, so when it stalls, work and backups stall with it. The causes are usually small: full storage, a frozen sync app, or a file name that Drive refuses to touch.

How to fix it

  1. Check your internet connection first.
  2. Check Google Drive storage is not full — drive.google.com shows the bar.
  3. Restart the Drive app (desktop) or the phone.
  4. On a computer, pause and resume syncing from the Drive icon near the clock.
  5. Check the file name — symbols like ? or * in names stop syncing.
Drive says 'storage full' but I barely saved anything — why?

Drive storage is shared with Gmail and Google Photos. Years of photos and emails quietly eat it — check the breakdown on one.google.com.

Get sync moving

  • Click the Drive icon and open its error list - it names the blocked file. Big files or names with characters like ? or : stall the whole queue.
  • Pause and resume sync - restarts stuck uploads.
  • Confirm all devices are signed into the same account - sync fails quietly between accounts.
  • Storage full stops everything - one.google.com shows what to clear.
  • Phone video files sometimes fail - upload those manually through the web.
  • A dead queue usually needs a reinstall of Drive for Desktop.
  • Antivirus and firewalls block the sync client - add it to the allowed list.

A trick that fixes stubborn sync: rename the file or folder that is stuck. The rename forces Drive to treat it as a new item and queue it fresh, which clears the error state that was blocking the whole queue.
