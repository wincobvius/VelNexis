How do I check who is using my WiFi

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 1, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 1, 2026

Your internet feels slow and you suspect that neighbors or unknown people are connected to your WiFi and eating your speed. Every device connected to your router is listed inside the router's settings page. Checking the list takes two minutes and tells you exactly how many devices are on your network.

Reading the router's list

  1. Open the router's settings: browser → 192.168.1.1 (or the address on the router's sticker) → log in with the sticker's admin credentials.
  2. Find the client list: called "Connected Devices", "DHCP Client List", "Attached Devices" or "Wireless Stations" depending on brand. It shows every connected device with a name, MAC address, and sometimes how long it has been connected.
  3. Count honestly against your household: phones, laptops, TVs, watches, consoles, smart bulbs — modern homes carry 15-25 legitimate devices. Identify yours by name where possible (the list shows device names like "Redmi-Note" or "LivingRoom-TV"). Unknown names deserve a closer look: one suspicious device is a problem; a list that matches your gadgets is not.

The smarter method for tricky cases

  1. The elimination test: turn off WiFi on your devices one by one (or all of them) and watch the list shrink — whatever remains connected when ALL your devices are off is not yours. That is the cleanest identification there is.
  2. Check for unknowns and act: found a freeloader? Change the WiFi password (see our guide) — one act evicts every device. For surgical removal, most routers let you block a specific MAC address in the wireless settings.

The expectation to set

Slow internet has many causes — and a full device list that matches your own home usually means the problem is the package or the hour, not a thief (see our evening-slowdown guide). But a stranger on your network is a genuine security issue regardless of speed: anyone on your WiFi sits inside your home network's front door. Check monthly; it costs nothing.
