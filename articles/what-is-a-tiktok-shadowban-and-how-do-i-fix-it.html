What is a TikTok shadowban and how do I fix it

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 22, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 22, 2026

Your videos suddenly stop getting views — from thousands down to double digits — without any notification or ban message. Creators call this a "shadowban": your content is quietly hidden from the For You page. It is not an official TikTok punishment; it is the algorithm reducing your reach after detecting rule-breaking content or spam-like behavior. Views usually come back if you clean up your account.

First, verify it is really a shadowban

  • One quiet video is normal — reach always varies.
  • A sudden, sustained collapse (80-90% down for days) on every video, with your followers still able to see posts, fits the pattern.
  • Check TikTok's own notices first: Account Status in settings shows real policy strikes — a visible strike is a different problem with a stated cause.

The cleanup that typically restores reach

  1. Delete or archive the problematic videos — anything with banned sounds, watermark-reused content from other platforms, flagged hashtags, or borderline content. The algorithm re-evaluates what remains, so the graveyard works against you.
  2. Remove spam-like behavior: follow/unfollow bursts, copy-paste comments, engagement-bait phrases ("follow back", "like chain"). It reads exactly like the bot patterns the system hunts.
  3. Take a short break — 24-72 hours of no posting, no mass activity. Accounts under review often pass it and return to normal reach afterwards.
  4. Come back clean: original content, trending-but-legitimate sounds, normal posting rhythm. Originality is the single strongest reach signal TikTok rewards.

The honest unknown

TikTok does not officially confirm shadowbans, so all of this is creator-community pattern-knowledge, not documented policy — treat the timelines as typical, not guaranteed. And if Account Status shows a real violation, fix that first: a documented strike has documented consequences.
