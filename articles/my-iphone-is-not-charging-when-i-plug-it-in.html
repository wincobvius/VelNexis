My iPhone is not charging when I plug it in

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 26, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 26, 2026

You connect the charger and nothing happens — no sound, no charging icon — or it charges only if you bend the cable at an angle. iPhone charging problems usually hide in three places: the cable/charger, the charging port full of pocket lint, or the battery/software. The angled-charging clue almost always means the port or cable is the culprit.

How to fix it

  1. Try another cable and charger first — most charging problems are broken cables.
  2. Clean the charging port gently with a dry toothpick or soft brush (lint gets stuck inside).
  3. Restart the iPhone.
  4. Check for debris or water warning in the port.
  5. If a "liquid detected" warning shows, let the port dry for hours. If it still fails, the port may be damaged — visit service.

Try these fixes in order

  • Check the cable and adapter first - borrow a known-good set. Charging problems are the cable far more often than the phone.
  • Look inside the charging port with a light. Pocket lint builds up at the bottom and blocks the plug. Pick it out gently with a wooden toothpick.
  • Force restart: press volume up, then volume down, then hold the side button until the Apple logo appears.
  • Let it charge for 30 minutes from a wall socket before judging. A deeply drained iPhone can show a dead screen for a while.
  • Avoid charging through a computer USB port or a car port - many give too little power for iPhones.
  • If the phone charges but stops at 80 percent, that is the built-in battery protection, not a fault. It protects an aging battery.
  • Still nothing? Check battery health in Settings > Battery. Below 80 percent health, a battery replacement fixes both charging and drain problems.
