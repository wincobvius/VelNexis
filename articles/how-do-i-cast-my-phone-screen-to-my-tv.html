How do I cast my phone screen to my TV

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 5, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 5, 2026

You want your phone's screen — a video, a game, photos — to appear on the big TV. Casting connects the two wirelessly, but people get stuck: the TV does not appear in the list, or casting works and then drops. The rules are few: same WiFi network, the right button on each system, and compatible hardware.

How to fix it

  1. Both devices must be on the same WiFi.
  2. Android: pull down quick settings → "Cast" or "Smart View" → choose the TV.
  3. iPhone: use AirPlay if the TV supports it (most new TVs do in settings).
  4. If the TV is not a smart TV, use a cheap Chromecast/Google TV stick or Fire TV stick.
  5. If casting fails, restart both devices and check both are on the same WiFi band name.

Cast the easy way

  • Android: the Cast or Screen Cast button lives in Quick Settings. Both the phone and the TV must be on the same WiFi - this is the number one reason casting fails.
  • Inside YouTube and Netflix, tap the cast icon - app casting is smoother than full screen mirroring.
  • No smart TV? A Chromecast or any Android TV stick adds casting to any TV with an HDMI port.
  • Samsung TVs support Smart View from Samsung phones; LG TVs support Screen Share.
  • Sound but no picture, or endless loading? Restart the router - casting uses the local network heavily.
  • iPhone to a non-Apple TV: use apps with built-in cast support like YouTube, or a mirror app. AirPlay needs an Apple TV or a compatible smart TV.
  • Keep the phone close to the router - mirrored screens stutter when phone WiFi drops to one bar.
