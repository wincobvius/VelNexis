My card keeps getting declined for online purchases

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 27, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 27, 2026

Your card works in shops but online payments keep declining. The decline message never tells you the reason, which is the real problem — but the cause always lives in one of three places: the card itself, your bank's settings, or the merchant's payment system. This order checks all three, cheapest first.

Check the card side

  1. Active and unexpired? Check the expiry date and, for a new card, whether it needs activation first.
  2. Funds or credit available? Include the site's currency conversion — a payment that looks within budget can exceed your limit after conversion.
  3. Typed correctly? Card number, expiry, CVV, and the name exactly as printed on the card. One transposed digit is the oldest decline in banking.

Check the bank side

  1. Ask the bank to enable e-commerce on the card. Many banks keep online and international transactions switched OFF by default — this is the single most common cause of "works in shops, fails online". The bank app often has the switch under card settings; otherwise one call: "enable e-commerce transactions on my card."
  2. Ask for the specific decline reason from the helpline. Their system shows exactly why each attempt failed — limit, currency, merchant category — which ends the guessing instantly.

Check the merchant side

  1. Only one site declining? Then the problem is usually theirs — their payment processor, their fraud filters, or a mismatch with your card type. Try another card there, and your card on another site; the pattern identifies the culprit.

Call your bank and ask them to whitelist the specific merchant category - many Pakistani banks block international online payments by default, and one phone call switches it on permanently.
