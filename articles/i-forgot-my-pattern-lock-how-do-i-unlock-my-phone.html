I forgot my pattern lock, how do I unlock my phone

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 25, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 25, 2026

You set a pattern, PIN or password on your phone and now you cannot remember it. After many wrong tries, the phone locks you out completely for some time. This is one of the most searched phone problems because it happens to everyone at least once. The real danger: without the correct lock or the Google account on the phone, you cannot enter your own phone. The fix works, but know that some methods delete the phone's data.

How to fix it

  1. Try "Find My Device" on Google. Open google.com/android/find on a computer.
  2. Sign in with the same Google account that is on the phone.
  3. Click "Erase Device". This deletes the lock but also deletes data.
  4. After erase, set up the phone again with your Google account.
  5. If this does not work, take the phone to the brand's service center with your purchase proof.

Unlock without the pattern

  • From any browser, open Android Find My Device (android.com/find), sign in with the phone's Google account, and Erase. Data goes, lock goes.
  • Samsung phones: findmymobile.samsung.com can unlock without erasing, if the account was registered before.
  • Older Androids show 'Forgot pattern' after several wrong tries - it asks for the Google account.
  • A backup PIN set alongside the pattern also unlocks after wrong attempts.
  • No Google account at all? Factory reset from recovery (power + volume buttons) is the last door.
  • After any reset, the phone asks for the previously synced Google account - so know that password before you start.
  • Data recovery from a pattern-locked phone is not realistically possible. That is the lock doing its job.

Before erasing, check if the phone's manufacturer service (Samsung Find My Mobile, Xiaomi Cloud) offers an 'unlock' option - several of them can remove the lock without deleting your data, which the Google route cannot.
