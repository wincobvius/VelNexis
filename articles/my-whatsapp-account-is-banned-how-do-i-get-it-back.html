My WhatsApp account is banned, how do I get it back

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 31, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 31, 2026

You open WhatsApp and see "This account is banned" or "Your account was banned" — you cannot send or receive anything. Bans mostly happen for using mod apps like GB WhatsApp, bulk messaging strangers, or getting reported by many people. Some bans are temporary, some permanent. There is an official appeal process, and honest users often get accounts back.

How to fix it

  1. Open WhatsApp. It will show "This account is banned".
  2. Tap "Request a review" inside the app if you see the button.
  3. Or email support@whatsapp.com. Write your number with country code and politely ask them to review.
  4. Bans usually happen for: using GB WhatsApp or mod apps, sending too many messages to strangers, or many people reporting you.
  5. Mod apps give a permanent ban risk. Always use the official WhatsApp.

Get unbanned the right way

  • Read the ban message. 'Temporarily banned' usually lifts by itself; a full ban needs an appeal.
  • On the ban screen, tap 'Request a review'. WhatsApp answers in-app appeals, often within a day or two.
  • Think about the trigger: bulk messages, forwarding viral chains, modified apps like GB WhatsApp, or mass reports. In your appeal, say what you will stop doing.
  • If you used a mod app, uninstall it, install official WhatsApp, then appeal - moving back to the official app helps your case.
  • Keep the appeal short and honest. No anger, no long story.
  • Do not register new numbers while banned - repeat behavior leads to permanent bans.
  • Turn on chat backup now, whatever happens - it protects your history for any outcome.

In the appeal, state what you will do differently in one sentence - reviewers read hundreds of denials and appeals, and the ones that show understanding get the second chance.
