My front camera is not working

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 3, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 3, 2026

The selfie camera does nothing — black screen in camera apps, failures in video calls, or the switch button does nothing. The front camera fails less often than the back one, so when it dies the cause is usually software (permissions, app cache) or a loosened module connector after a drop.

The diagnostic order

  1. Test in a video-call app first (WhatsApp, Duo): if the front camera works THERE but not in the camera app, the problem is the camera app — clear its data or reinstall. Working anywhere means the hardware is alive.
  2. Black in EVERY app? Restart the phone. A camera service crash blanks the front camera everywhere; a full restart resets it.
  3. Permissions audit (Settings → Apps → the app → Permissions): camera permission granted, and on iPhone check Settings → Privacy → Camera — the toggle for the specific app. A flipped-off permission produces black screens, not error messages.
  4. Clear camera app data (Android) / offload-reinstall the app — corrupted app data is the biggest software cause.
  5. Safe-mode / other-user test (Android): camera in Safe Mode rules out a third-party app squatting on the camera.

The hardware signs

  • Front camera dead everywhere, in every app, after every restart — especially following a drop, a repair, or water: the module or its ribbon connector.
  • Front camera works but is BLACK-AND-WHITE, or grainy, or half-blocked: connector or sensor damage, same repair path.
  • The self-test: dial the code your brand uses for hardware diagnostics (or a diagnostic app from the maker), which exercises the front camera directly.

Before the repair visit

Back the phone up — and note that a front-camera module replacement is one of the cheaper phone repairs. If a repair shop recently opened the phone (screen or battery), return to THEM first: a front camera that died right after an unrelated repair is a re-seatable connector 90% of the time, and a good shop fixes it on the spot, free.
