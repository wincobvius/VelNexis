I dropped my phone and now the camera is not working

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 12, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 12, 2026

After a drop, your camera stopped working — black screen, error messages, or a rattling sound from the lens area. Drops damage cameras in specific ways: the module's connector loosens, the focus mechanism jams, the lens cover glass cracks. Some drop damage responds to restarts; most needs a technician.

How to fix it

  1. Restart the phone first — drops sometimes loosen connections that a restart resets.
  2. Gently tap the phone near the camera area (light taps only) — sometimes it reseats a loose connector.
  3. Check the lens glass — a cracked cover glass over the lens needs replacement (cheap).
  4. If the camera shows a black screen or the phone vibrates when opening the camera, the module is damaged.
  5. Camera module replacement is a common, moderately priced repair.

What to do after the drop

  • Restart the phone first. A hard knock can freeze the camera app like any crash - sometimes it is only software.
  • Test in Safe Mode. If the camera works there, an app is blocking it; if it fails there too, the fall damaged the hardware.
  • Look at the lens from an angle under light - a loose lens glass or a rattle means physical damage.
  • Tap the camera icons to switch lenses. Often only one lens is damaged and the others still work.
  • If photos are blurry but the app opens, the lens may have shifted - a repair shop can often fix just the camera for much less than a screen.
  • Back up your photos now. Camera damage sometimes comes with other problems that show up days later.

Photograph the damage immediately - screen, lens area, any rattle. If a warranty claim or insurance claim follows, photos taken on day one carry weight that later photos do not.
