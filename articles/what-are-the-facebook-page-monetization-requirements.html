What are the Facebook page monetization requirements

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 25, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 25, 2026

You run a Facebook page with growing content and want to start earning from Facebook's ads and tools. But Facebook monetization has specific, numbered requirements — followers, watch time, content rules — and your Professional Dashboard shows some tools as ineligible. Understanding exactly which numbers unlock what saves months of confused posting.

The two main programs

  1. In-stream ads (ads inside your videos): historically requires 5,000 followers and 60,000 minutes of viewed content in the last 60 days (plus 5+ active videos) — Facebook adjusts these thresholds by country and over time. The Professional Dashboard shows YOUR live numbers and progress bars.
  2. Reels/Performance bonus and other programs: requirements and availability change frequently — the Dashboard's "Monetization" tab lists what your page qualifies for TODAY, which beats any article's numbers (including this one — treat these as the ballpark, and the Dashboard as the truth).

The universal rules that block pages regardless of numbers

  1. Content rules apply before numbers do: original content only (reused/compilations fail), no clickbait, no engagement bait ("tag 3 friends"), and page history free of strikes. A page with 100k followers and reused videos earns nothing — the policy check is independent of the popularity check.
  2. The 30-days-active requirement: pages go inactive in the system after long posting gaps — steady posting matters as much as totals.
  3. Eligible country + payment setup: Pakistan is on Facebook's monetization map for several programs; completing the payout setup (bank/Payoneer details, tax info) is part of eligibility, not a step after it.

The practical path

Open Professional Dashboard → Monetization: it shows each tool, your eligibility status, and the exact missing requirement. Build toward the closest unlock, keep content original, and re-check monthly — thresholds move, and pages sitting at the boundary qualify when they drop.
