I forgot my Windows password, how do I log in

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 6, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 6, 2026

You are stuck at the Windows login screen, password gone from memory. The fix depends entirely on one thing — what type of account you have — and you can tell from the login screen itself: an email address shown as the username means a Microsoft account; a plain username means an old-style local account.

Note: The last option in this guide resets the PC. "Keep my files" spares personal files but removes apps and settings; "remove everything" wipes the drive. Try the recovery steps first.

Microsoft account (email as username)

  1. Click "Reset password" on the login screen, or open account.live.com/password/reset on your phone. Both do the same thing: verify your identity (via email, SMS or authenticator) and set a new password.
  2. Sign in on the PC with the new password once the PC is online.

Local account (plain username)

  1. Another account on the same PC? Sign into it (sign-in options → other account), then change your password from Settings → Accounts.
  2. A password reset disk you made earlier? This is the moment it exists for — the "Reset password" link on the login screen walks you through it.
  3. Otherwise, local accounts have no online reset — by design. A technician with the right tools can unlock it without data loss, which is the safe route.

Last resort: reset the PC

Hold Shift while clicking Restart on the login screen → Troubleshoot → Reset this PC → "Keep my files". Apps and settings go; documents survive. Afterwards, set up a recovery method you will not lose: a Microsoft account with updated recovery info, or a reset disk on a labeled USB.
