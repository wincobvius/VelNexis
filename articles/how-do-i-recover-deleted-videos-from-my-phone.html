How do I recover deleted videos from my phone

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 25, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 25, 2026

Videos disappeared from your phone — deleted by a cleaning app, a wrong tap, or a child's curious fingers. Videos are the biggest, most precious files on phones, and losing them hurts. The recovery path runs through the gallery's trash folders first, then cloud backups, and only then recovery software — each step has real chances.

How to fix it

  1. Check the Gallery's "Trash/Recently deleted" folder — items stay 30 days.
  2. Check Google Photos trash the same way.
  3. If not there, stop using the phone for big downloads (new data overwrites deleted videos).
  4. Connect the phone to a PC and try recovery software that supports phone storage — success is limited on modern phones.
  5. Best protection: turn on Google Photos backup so this never happens again.

Where deleted videos hide

  • Check 'Recently Deleted' in your gallery app - phones keep deleted photos and videos for 30 days before truly erasing them.
  • Open Google Photos > Library > Trash. Items stay there 30 days too, if backup was on.
  • Check cloud backups: Google Photos, Samsung Cloud, or your phone brand's cloud - a video deleted on the phone may still sit in the cloud.
  • Stop recording new video until you recover what you need. New videos overwrite the space where old ones still sit.
  • If none of that works, connect the phone to a PC and run a recovery tool like DiskDigger.
  • Videos received on WhatsApp are also saved in the WhatsApp folder on your storage - look there before assuming they are gone.

Check the WhatsApp folder first before assuming loss - videos you ever sent or received sit in the WhatsApp Media directory and survive many deletions that clear the gallery.
