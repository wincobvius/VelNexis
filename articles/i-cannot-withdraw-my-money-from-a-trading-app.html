I cannot withdraw my money from a trading app

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 23, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 23, 2026

Your trading or investment app shows a profit, but when you try to withdraw, it fails — errors, pending forever, or new conditions appear: "deposit more to unlock withdrawal". This is the single clearest scam signal in the online money world. Real platforms let you withdraw; fake ones raise walls. Knowing which situation you are in decides your next move.

How to fix it

  1. First check: did you complete the app's verification (ID, photo)?
  2. Check the minimum withdrawal amount and any trading volume rules.
  3. Screenshot the error message. Contact the app's official support with it.
  4. If the app asks you to deposit more to unlock withdrawal — it is a scam. Do not deposit.
  5. Report scam apps to FIA Cyber Crime and your bank to block future payments to them.

Handle a stuck withdrawal

  • Check the withdrawal status inside the app. 'Processing' for 1 to 3 business days is normal; 'rejected' shows a reason.
  • Verify your KYC documents are approved. Most withdrawal blocks are incomplete verification, not lost money.
  • Check the minimum withdrawal amount and fees - requests below the minimum sit forever.\- If the app gave you a bonus, check its conditions. Bonuses usually lock withdrawals until a trading volume is reached.
  • Screenshot everything: balance, withdrawal request, messages. Tickets with screenshots get answered faster.
  • Contact support in the app first, then the official email, and always quote the transaction reference number.
  • One honest warning: if the app demands a 'fee' to release your money, or pushes you to deposit more in order to withdraw, that is a classic scam pattern. Stop depositing.

Photograph your balance and every withdrawal attempt the day trouble starts. If the app later changes numbers or deletes history - which happens - your dated screenshots are the only record that exists.
