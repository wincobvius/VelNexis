Play Store is not downloading apps

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 23, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 23, 2026

You tap "Install" but the download never starts, or it stays on "Pending" forever. Sometimes it shows an error code and stops. This happens because of a weak internet connection, a full storage, a problem in the Play Store app's saved data, or wrong date and time on the phone. It is a very common problem and is almost always fixed by clearing the Play Store's cache. It has nothing to do with your Google account being blocked in most cases.

How to fix it

  1. Check your internet. Open a website to test.
  2. Clear Play Store cache: Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear Cache.
  3. Also clear cache of "Google Play Services".
  4. Check date and time. Set them to automatic.
  5. Restart the phone and try again.

Get downloads moving

  • Check date and time first: Settings > System > Date and time. A wrong clock breaks the Play Store connection silently.
  • Clear cache and data for Play Store AND Download Manager (Settings > Apps > Show system apps).
  • Remove and re-add your Google account, sign back in, and try again.
  • Check Play Store > profile > Settings > Network preferences - if downloads are set to WiFi only, they wait forever on mobile data.
  • Free up storage - downloads stop completely when storage is full.
  • Uninstall Play Store updates (Settings > Apps > Google Play Store). It rebuilds itself clean.
  • If downloads are stuck on 'pending', clear the queue - stop one download instead of starting ten at once.

Quick recap

  • Correct date and time first - wrong clocks break Play silently.
  • Clear cache for Play Store and Download Manager.
  • Check the WiFi-only download setting.
  • Full storage stops downloads completely.

Account-level fixes

  • Remove and re-add your Google account (Settings > Accounts). Sync bugs inside the account block downloads in ways that clearing cache never touches. Your data stays in the cloud — this only rebuilds the connection.
  • Check Play Protect: Play Store menu > Play Protect > settings gear. A scan stuck in error can make the whole store misbehave. Toggle it off, restart the phone, toggle it on.
  • A corrupted payment profile breaks paid downloads. In Play Store payment methods, remove the dead card even if you only download free apps — the store checks the profile anyway.
  • Family accounts sometimes hit approval locks: a child profile waiting for a parent's approval shows up as endless pending. Check family settings at families.google.com.

If the store itself won't open

When the Play Store crashes on launch, its download queue is usually corrupt. Settings > Apps > Google Play Store > Storage > Clear data — a bigger hammer than clearing cache, and safe: you just sign back in. If that fails, uninstall Play Store updates. The store restores itself to factory state and re-updates within minutes, rebuilding the broken parts from scratch.
