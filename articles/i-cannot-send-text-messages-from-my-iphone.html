I cannot send text messages from my iPhone

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 21, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 21, 2026

Your messages fail — green bubbles show "Not Delivered", or messages to non-iPhone users never arrive. iPhones have two messaging systems running side by side: iMessage (blue, Apple-to-Apple, uses internet) and SMS (green, uses your network). Each system fails for different reasons, and knowing which one broke halves the troubleshooting.

Step 1 — Read the bubble color

  1. Blue bubble failing → iMessage, an internet problem: check Wi-Fi/mobile data, toggle airplane mode for 10 seconds, and check Apple's system status page for iMessage outages (rare, but real).
  2. Green bubble failing → SMS, a network/SIM problem: check signal bars, and try calling someone — if calls fail too, it is the SIM/network, not the phone's settings.
  3. Messages to ONE person only failing → probably not you at all: their phone is off, out of coverage, or has blocked you. Test with a message to a different contact.

Step 2 — The standard fixes

  1. Toggle iMessage off and on: Settings → Messages → iMessage off, wait 30 seconds, on. This re-registers your number with Apple's servers — the classic cure after activation hiccups.
  2. Check your Apple ID and "Send & Receive" list (Settings → Messages → Send & Receive): your phone number must appear and be ticked. If it is missing, sign out of Apple ID and back in.
  3. Restart the phone — resets the modem properly. For the stubborn case: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Reset → Reset Network Settings (note: this clears saved Wi-Fi passwords, so know them first).

The MMS variant

Pictures to Android users failing while text works? That is MMS — check Settings → Messages → MMS Messaging is ON, and that mobile data is enabled (MMS refuses to travel over Wi-Fi or with data off, however strong the signal).
