My iPhone keeps showing 'Searching' and never finds the network

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 16, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 16, 2026

Where the signal bars should be, your iPhone shows "Searching…" — endlessly. No calls, no data, nothing. This happens when the SIM is not seated properly, the network settings are corrupted, the SIM itself is dead, or the phone's antenna failed. A step-by-step check finds which one it is in minutes.

The ladder, rung by rung

  1. Airplane mode on for 15 seconds, then off. The cheapest reset of the radios; fixes the momentary modem confusion that follows basements and flights.
  2. Restart the iPhone properly — hold volume + side button, slide to power off, turn back on. A full power cycle resets the modem in a way airplane mode cannot.
  3. Reseat the SIM: eject the tray (paperclip in the side hole), take the SIM out, check it for visible damage or misalignment, put it back firmly. While it is out, note the tray's orientation — a half-seated tray produces exactly this symptom.
  4. Test the SIM in another phone. Works there → the problem is your iPhone. Still "Searching" there → the SIM is dead or deactivated; your network operator replaces SIMs cheaply and reactivates your number on the new one.
  5. Reset Network Settings: Settings → General → Transfer or Reset → Reset → Reset Network Settings. This rebuilds the phone's network configuration from scratch. It clears saved Wi-Fi passwords, so have those ready.
  6. Check for a carrier settings update (Settings → General → About — a popup appears if one is pending) and an iOS update.

When it is the phone

If another SIM also shows "Searching" in your iPhone, the antenna or its connector is the suspect — especially after a drop or a repair by a non-Apple shop. That is a hardware visit. And if it started after a liquid incident, the timeline is your diagnosis: corrosion does exactly this, and sooner is cheaper.
