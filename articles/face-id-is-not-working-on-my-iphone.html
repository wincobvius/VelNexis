Face ID is not working on my iPhone

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 11, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 11, 2026

You look at your phone and it refuses to unlock — "Face ID not recognized" — or it says Face ID is unavailable. Causes range from simple (dirty sensor, low eyes in the morning, post-restart state) to real (damaged TrueDepth camera after a drop). Re-enrolling your face fixes a surprising share of cases.

How to fix it

  1. Check nothing covers the TrueDepth camera (screen protector dirt, case).
  2. Settings → Face ID and Passcode → Reset Face ID, then set it up again.
  3. Set up Face ID again with good light and eyes open.
  4. After restart, you must type the passcode once — Face ID starts after that. This is normal.
  5. If "Face ID not available" keeps showing, visit service — the sensor may be damaged.

Fix Face ID

  • Clean the camera area (the notch) with a dry cloth - a screen protector or dust over the sensors is the most common cause.
  • Go to Settings > Face ID and Passcode, tap 'Set Up an Alternate Appearance' and re-scan your face in good light.
  • Turn Face ID off and on again for iPhone Unlock - a simple settings refresh.
  • Make sure your face is not covered: mask, cap shadow, or the phone held too low.
  • Install iOS updates - Face ID bugs are usually fixed in the next patch.
  • If settings shows 'Face ID is not available', a fall may have damaged the sensor. That needs a service center.

Quick recap

  • Clean the notch area and check the screen protector cutout.
  • Re-enroll with an alternate appearance in good light.
  • 'Face ID not available' in settings points to hardware - service.
  • Update iOS before assuming damage.
