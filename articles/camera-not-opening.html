My Phone Camera Is Not Opening at All

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Sep 10, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Sep 10, 2026

You tap the camera icon and nothing happens — the app flashes and closes, or shows a black screen forever. The camera is the most used phone feature after calls, so this feels like losing a limb. Causes run from trivial (another app holding the camera hostage) to serious (a damaged camera module). Most cases end at the second step of the fix list.

How to fix it

  1. Restart the phone — this fixes most camera problems by freeing the camera from the app that grabbed it.
  2. Close every app running in the background, especially video calls, Snapchat-type apps and flashlight apps — one of them may be holding the camera.
  3. Clear the camera app's cache: Settings → Apps → Camera → Storage → Clear cache.
  4. Check camera permissions: Settings → Apps → Camera → Permissions → allow camera.
  5. If the camera still shows black, test in safe mode. If it works there, a third-party app is the culprit; if not, the camera module likely needs repair.
Why does restarting fix the camera so often?

Android allows only one app to own the camera at a time. When a buggy app grabs it and never lets go, the camera app gets a black screen — a restart sets it free.

Quick recap

  • Restart first - a frozen camera service is the most common cause.
  • Clear the camera app's cache and check free storage - a full phone blocks the camera silently.
  • Test in safe mode: camera works there means another app (often a flashlight or cleaner) is holding the camera.
  • 'Camera busy' error across all apps after a drop points to hardware.
