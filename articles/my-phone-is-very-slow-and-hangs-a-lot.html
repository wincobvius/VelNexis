My phone is very slow and hangs a lot

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 21, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 21, 2026

Your phone takes a long time to open apps, the screen freezes while typing, and everything feels stuck. This happens when the storage is almost full, too many apps run in the background, or the phone is old with a weak processor. Heavy apps like Facebook, Instagram and games make it worse on budget phones. Hanging also happens when the phone has not been restarted for many days. If it hangs while calling or opening the camera, it needs fixing soon.

How to fix it

  1. Restart the phone. This fixes most hanging.
  2. Close apps running in the background.
  3. Delete unused apps and old files. Keep at least 20% storage free.
  4. Clear cache of heavy apps like WhatsApp and Facebook.
  5. If nothing works, back up data and do a factory reset.

Speed it up for real

  • Restart the phone properly at least once a week. Weeks of uptime make phones sluggish.
  • Check storage in Settings > Storage. Phones slow down badly when storage is over 90 percent full - delete big videos and apps.
  • Remove apps you never open, especially 'cleaner' and 'battery saver' apps - they run constantly and slow everything.
  • Turn off or reduce animations (Developer options on Android) - the phone feels faster instantly.
  • Update the phone software. Big slowness after a version change is often fixed in the follow-up patch.
  • Check battery health if the phone is more than 2 years old. A worn battery makes the system slow itself down to survive.
  • Avoid live wallpapers and too many home screen widgets - small savings that add up on older phones.
  • If it is still slow after all this, back up everything and do a factory reset. It feels like a new phone.
