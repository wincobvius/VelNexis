My emails are stuck and not sending

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 28, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 28, 2026

You press Send, the email does not go, and it sits in Drafts or shows "not sent" with a retry icon. The receiver never gets it, and you may not even notice for days. Stuck emails almost always have one of three causes: a broken internet connection, a wrong email address, or an attachment that is too big to pass through.

The three causes, checked in order

  1. Connection first. Open a random webpage to test — a Wi-Fi network that shows connected but is actually dead is the most common invisible cause. Switch to mobile data (or vice versa) and retry from the stuck email.
  2. The address. One wrong character and the email bounces or sits undelivered. Check the full address character by character — especially autocomplete suggestions that picked a similar-but-wrong contact.
  3. The attachment. Anything near or over 25 MB will not send through Gmail's regular attach. Check the file size; if it is the culprit, upload to Google Drive and send the link instead.

Then clear the blockage

  1. Open the Outbox/Drafts and retry the stuck mail manually — in the Gmail app, pull the email open and tap Send again. A mail stuck from a moment of bad connection often goes on the second attempt.
  2. Still stuck? Delete it and rewrite it. Corrupted stuck emails sometimes refuse every retry; a fresh copy sends cleanly. Copy the text out first so you do not retype it.
  3. If NOTHING sends over days: remove the account from the mail app and add it back — app sync tokens expire and break sending while receiving still works, which is exactly the confusing symptom you are seeing.

The habit that prevents it

Watch the send confirmation for a second after pressing Send — the tiny "Sent" beat is cheaper than discovering a week later that the job application never left your phone.
