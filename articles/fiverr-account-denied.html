My Fiverr Account Was Denied — How Do I Get It Back?

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Sep 9, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Sep 9, 2026

You signed up on Fiverr and during registration your account was denied, or it got blocked later. For new freelancers this is heartbreaking, because Fiverr is usually the first stop for earning online. Denials happen for fixable reasons: unclear ID photos, mismatched information, multiple accounts, or country and payment issues. Understanding the reason is the whole game.

How to fix it

  1. Fiverr sends the reason by email. Read it carefully.
  2. Common reasons: fake information, more than one account, wrong ID, or country/payment mismatch.
  3. Contact Fiverr support (support.fiverr.com) and politely ask them to review with your correct details.
  4. If your ID was the issue, re-submit a clear, original ID card photo.
  5. Never make a second account to bypass a ban — Fiverr blocks devices, and it makes things worse.
Can I open a new Fiverr account after a denial?

Not on the same device or payment details — Fiverr links them and the new account gets denied too. Fix the original account through support instead.

Appeal the denial properly

  • Read the denial email - it names the reason: profile issues, multiple accounts, or failed verification.
  • Submit the appeal form once, with short factual answers. Multiple appeals hurt the case.
  • Failed ID or video verification? Redo it exactly as asked: good light, plain background, document fully visible.
  • One account per person, ever. Family members selling too need separate devices and payment methods, or Fiverr links them.
  • Denied after appeal? Wait for the allowed reapply window - forced new accounts get banned on sight.
  • Meanwhile, build the same skills on Upwork and direct clients - one platform is never a career.
  • Most denials trace to a Terms of Service rule the seller never read. Read it once, fully.
