I reset my phone without a backup, can I get my photos back

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 22, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 22, 2026

You (or a shop) factory reset the phone, everything wiped — and there was no backup. The gallery, the chats, the contacts: gone? Before grief, check the cloud: photos have a habit of being synced without owners realizing it. Google Photos, WhatsApp backups and other clouds often hold copies people forgot existed.

How to fix it

  1. Check Google Photos first — even after reset, photos synced to the cloud come back on sign in.
  2. Check WhatsApp backup — chats and media restore if a backup existed.
  3. Check other cloud apps (Drive, Telegram saved files, email attachments).
  4. If nothing was backed up, recovery from the phone itself is very hard after a factory reset — the space is marked free.
  5. Some recovery shops try deep chip recovery — costly with no guarantee.

What are the real chances

  • Check every cloud: Google Photos, Drive, Samsung Cloud, Dropbox, WhatsApp chat backup. A surprising amount survives in the cloud without people knowing it was ever on.
  • Check other devices still signed in: a tablet, an old phone, a PC browser. Photos, contacts and notes sync there automatically.
  • Ask family and friends - photos you sent on WhatsApp are saved on their phones too.
  • Check your email for attachments you ever sent to yourself or others.
  • If the factory reset truly completed with no backup anywhere, phone recovery tools cannot help - internal storage is encrypted after a reset.
  • Lesson for next time: turn on Google backup today (Settings > Google > Backup). It is free and works quietly in the background.

If your phone was ever linked to a Google or Samsung account, log into that account from any browser and check photos and contacts sections - sync often preserved more than people expect, even without a manual backup.
