Browser Problems

Files are not downloading from Chrome

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 8, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 8, 2026

You click a download link and nothing happens — no download starts, it fails instantly with "Failed", or it stalls at some percent forever. Downloads fail from full disks, broken links, browser cache issues, or antivirus quietly blocking files it does not like. Each cause leaves a different clue in the download bar.

Read the clue first

  • Nothing at all happens → usually the link, the popup blocker, or a frozen tab
  • "Failed — Virus detected" / "Blocked" → antivirus or Chrome's own protection
  • Stalls at a percentage forever → connection instability or the server
  • "Disk full" → exactly what it says

The fixes by cause

  1. Check disk space on C: (or your Downloads drive). A full disk silently kills every download. Freeing a few GB fixes the whole category.
  2. Test another file from another site. One dead link is the site's problem, not yours — right-click the link → "Save link as…" as a workaround, or try later.
  3. Ctrl+Shift+Delete → clear cache (last hour is enough), then retry. Corrupted cache breaks download handshakes in ways that look like dead links.
  4. Antivirus flags: if you KNOW the file is safe, your security software's quarantine/downloads list lets you restore or allow it — check the antivirus app, not the browser. Chrome's own "Keep" button appears on the download's detail row.
  5. Stuck at a percentage: pause and resume from the download bar (Chrome finally supports it), or switch networks — flaky Wi-Fi is the most common invisible cause.
  6. The nuclear-but-safe option: try the same download in another browser or in incognito (extensions off). If incognito works, an extension — usually a download manager or ad blocker — is your culprit.

One honest warning

"Blocked – dangerous" downloads deserve a second thought, not just a "Keep" reflex. Cracked software and "free" premium tools are exactly what those blocks exist for.
