Pop up ads keep coming on my phone, how do I stop them

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 2, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 2, 2026

Ads appear on your screen even when no app is open — on the home screen, over other apps, or full screen ads that cover everything. This almost always means a bad app is installed on your phone, usually a free game, cleaner, torch or VPN app from outside the Play Store. These apps show ads to earn money from your phone. The ads also use your data and drain the battery.

How to fix it

  1. Check recently installed apps. Uninstall any unknown app, especially cleaners or free games.
  2. Settings → Apps → the suspicious app → Uninstall.
  3. Run a Play Protect scan: Play Store → Profile → Play Protect → Scan.
  4. Turn off notifications from unknown apps: long press the ad notification → turn off.
  5. If ads still come, scan with a trusted antivirus app.

Stop the pop-ups

  • Find the culprit: when a pop-up appears, check recent apps and recently installed apps - uninstall the one that showed it.
  • Chrome settings > Site settings > Pop-ups and redirects > Blocked.
  • Check notification permissions: sites you accidentally allowed send 'ads' as notifications - block them in Chrome notification settings.
  • Delete 'cleaner', 'flashlight' and 'free VPN' style apps - these serve lock screen and full screen ads.
  • Run a Play Protect scan - adware apps get flagged.
  • If the browser redirects to junk pages, clear Chrome's cache and data.
  • Never tap the X on fake virus warnings - press Home instead, then deal with the app.

Quick recap

  • Uninstall the newest apps first - the pop-up app is almost always the most recent install.
  • Block site notifications in Chrome settings.
  • Delete cleaner, flashlight and free VPN apps.
  • Fake virus warnings get closed with Home, never with their X button.
