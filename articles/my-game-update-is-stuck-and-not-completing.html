My game update is stuck and not completing

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 26, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 26, 2026

A game update crawls — stuck at 0%, 99%, or "queued" forever — while you sit watching a progress bar refuse to move. Stuck updates come from paused-then-forgotten downloads, server overload on patch days, full storage, or console network glitches. Nearly all of them break loose with the same three moves.

The three moves

  1. Pause and resume the update. Genuinely — a paused queue that never resumed is the most common stuck update, and pressing pause-then-play kicks the download manager back to life.
  2. Restart the console/launcher fully (consoles: full power off, not rest mode; PC: close the launcher from the tray entirely and reopen). The download service, not the download, is usually what is stuck.
  3. Check free storage before anything else: a 40 GB update needs 40+ GB free (sometimes double, temporarily). A full drive freezes updates at a random percentage with no clear error — clear space, resume.

If the three moves fail

  1. Patch-day reality: on launch days and big patches, the publisher's download servers are simply hammered — speeds of 0.1 Mbps and stalls at 99% are worldwide, not yours. Check Downdetector or the community: patience or 3 AM are the actual fixes.
  2. Clear the download cache (PC): Steam → Settings → Downloads → Clear Download Cache — fixes stuck-at-0% on PC more often than anything else. Consoles: rebuild the connection by testing network settings → then retry.
  3. DNS switch (8.8.8.8) helps when downloads stall while the browser works — ISP routing to CDN servers is a real thing.
  4. The queue-blocker: other downloads or updates queued ahead (system updates, other games) hold your update back. Pause everything else, keep the one you want.

Console-specific

Rest mode is generally FINE for downloads (PlayStation and Xbox download in low-power mode by design) — but a stuck update sometimes needs the console awake and on the download page to finish. If it stalls overnight every night, let it run in foreground once.
