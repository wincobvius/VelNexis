How do I block unwanted emails in Gmail

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 9, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 9, 2026

A sender keeps mailing you — promotions, a mailing list, or someone annoying — and deleting each email by hand is endless. Gmail has a block feature that sends everything from a chosen sender straight to spam automatically, forever. One click today saves you from every future email from them.

How to fix it

  1. Open one of the sender's emails.
  2. Click the three dots at the top right of the email (not of the inbox).
  3. Click "Block [sender]" and confirm. From that moment, everything they send lands in Spam automatically — you will never see it in the inbox again.
  4. For newsletters you once wanted: instead of blocking, use "Unsubscribe" in the same menu — Gmail now puts an unsubscribe link at the top of most marketing mail, which is one click and cleaner than blocking.
  5. For a whole group of senders at once, create a filter: Gmail search bar → show search options (sliders icon) → type the address or keyword in "From" → "Create filter" → choose "Delete it" or "Skip inbox". Future matches get handled automatically.

Unblocking, and the one thing blocking cannot do

Blocked someone by mistake? Settings → "Filters and Blocked Addresses" → Unblock. And note: blocking sends mail to Spam, where it auto-deletes after 30 days — so if you are waiting for a possible genuine email from that sender (a company, a person you argued with), check Spam before assuming it never came.

Does the sender know I blocked them?

No — Gmail sends no notification. Their emails simply vanish into your Spam folder silently.
