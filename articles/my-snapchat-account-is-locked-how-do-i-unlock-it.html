My Snapchat account is locked, how do I unlock it

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 23, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 23, 2026

Snapchat will not let you in — it says your account is locked. This happens when Snapchat suspects someone hacked the account, when you used a third-party app or plugin with Snapchat, or when you broke its rules. Some locks are temporary (a day), and some need action from you. Snapchat has an official unlock page on its website.

The unlock attempt

  1. Go to accounts.snapchat.com and log in. If the account shows as locked, the page explains the reason category and offers an Unlock button. Some locks simply clear 24 hours after they appear — if Unlock says "try later", that is the message.
  2. Remove every third-party app before unlocking. Unofficial Snapchat tools (savers, "ghost mode" apps, modified clients) are the single most common permanent-lock cause. Unlocking while still using them re-locks the account — often permanently.
  3. Change your password from the same page if the lock followed suspicious activity — it signals to Snapchat that control is back with you.
  4. Verify your email and phone in account settings — an unverifiable account looks like a bot account to their systems.

If it stays locked

The support form on the same site handles appeals: explain plainly what happened, what third-party app was involved (honesty helps — they can see it anyway), and ask for review. Legitimate first-time offenders with clean appeals are routinely unlocked; repeat automated abuse is not.

What "permanently locked" means

A permanent lock cannot be appealed in most cases — the account and its memories are gone. Which makes the prevention list short and strict: official app only, no plugins, password you use nowhere else.
