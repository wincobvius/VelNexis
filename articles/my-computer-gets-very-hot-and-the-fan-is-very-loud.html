My computer gets very hot and the fan is very loud

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 11, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 11, 2026

The laptop becomes burning hot, the fan roars like a jet, and the PC slows down while hot. Computers make heat by design, and the fan's job is to push it out — but when dust blocks the vents or the cooling paste inside dries up, heat gets trapped. Heat is dangerous for computers: it makes them slow and shortens the life of every part inside.

How to fix it

  1. Clean the air vents with a soft brush or compressed air. Dust blocks cooling.
  2. Do not use the laptop on a bed or pillow — keep the bottom open to air.
  3. Check Task Manager — if some program uses 100% CPU, close it.
  4. Update the graphics driver — old drivers overwork the chip.
  5. If it is always hot and slow, the thermal paste inside may be dried. A repair shop can clean and re-paste it cheaply.

Cool it down

  • Dust first: a blower through the vents drops temperatures massively. Do it every 6 months.
  • Check the fans actually spin - a dead fan makes the survivor scream. Replacements are cheap.
  • Give it air: no closed cabinets, nothing blocking vents, space behind the PC.
  • Laptops belong on hard flat surfaces, never beds or pillows - cloth blocks fans completely.
  • Check Task Manager for a runaway app at 100 percent CPU - one bad app sounds like a jet.
  • CPUs older than 3-4 years need fresh thermal paste - a shop repaste is cheap and drops temperatures hard.
  • Sustained 90-plus degrees shortens every part's life. Do not ignore a constantly loud fan.

Once a year, open the case and blow the dust out - or have a shop do it for a small fee. The difference in temperature and noise is dramatic, and it extends the life of every component inside.
