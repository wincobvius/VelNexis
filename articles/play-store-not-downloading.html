Google Play Store Not Downloading or Updating Apps — Fixes

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Sep 11, 2026 · 5 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Sep 11, 2026

You tap Install on an app, the bar spins, and nothing downloads — or your existing apps refuse to update. A stuck Play Store is one of the most common Android problems, and it almost always comes down to five things: a confused download queue, a full cache, no free storage, a payment method that needs attention, or Play Store itself waiting for an update.

How to fix it

Note: Some steps below reset settings or clear an app's data, so you may need to sign in again afterwards. Keep your passwords handy before you start.
  1. Cancel the stuck download, then restart the phone and try again — this alone fixes a surprising share of cases.
  2. Clear the Play Store's cache: Settings → Apps → Google Play Store → Storage → Clear cache (and Clear data if needed — you will not lose your apps).
  3. Check free storage — the Play Store silently refuses to download when storage is nearly full. Keep at least 1-2 GB free.
  4. Check the download manager: Settings → Apps → Download Manager (or Downloads) → Enable, if it was disabled.
  5. Remove and re-add your Google account: Settings → Accounts → Google → Remove, then add it back. This resets the store's connection properly.
Downloads only fail on WiFi, mobile data works — why?

That points to the router or the ISP blocking Google's download servers. Test another WiFi network; if it works there, restart your router or change its DNS to 8.8.8.8.

Will clearing Play Store data delete my installed apps?

No. It only resets the store's settings — your apps, and your app list tied to your Google account, all stay safe.
