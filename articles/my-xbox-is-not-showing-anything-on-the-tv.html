My Xbox is not showing anything on the TV

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 22, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 22, 2026

Your Xbox is running — you hear it, its light is on — but the TV screen shows nothing: black, "No Signal", or the wrong input. This display chain has three links: console, cable, TV. Most "broken" Xboxes are actually fine; the fault hides in the cable, the TV's input setting, or a display setting the console remembered from another TV.

How to fix it

  1. Check the TV is on the correct HDMI input (press the source/input button).
  2. Reseat the HDMI cable at both ends, or try another HDMI cable and port.
  3. Do a display reset: with the console on, hold the power button 10 seconds to shut down, then press and hold power + eject until you hear two beeps — this boots at low resolution.
  4. Try the console on another TV to see which device is faulty.
  5. Check TV settings — some TVs need "HDMI UHD color" or "enhanced HDMI" turned on for consoles.

Get the picture back

  • Press Source or Input on the TV remote and cycle every HDMI - 'no signal' is often just the wrong input.
  • Power drain the console: unplug 60 seconds, hold its power button 10 seconds, replug.
  • Try an HDMI cable that works on another device, and a different TV port.
  • Low-resolution boot: hold Power and Eject until two beeps - it starts at 640x480, which any TV can show. Then fix the display settings.
  • 4K and 120Hz settings silently fail on some TVs - the low-res boot above gets you back in.
  • Lights on, no picture on any TV or cable: the HDMI chip failed - a known repair at service centers.
