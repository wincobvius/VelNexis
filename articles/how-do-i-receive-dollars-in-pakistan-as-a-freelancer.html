How do I receive dollars in Pakistan as a freelancer

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Jul 31, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Jul 31, 2026

You are earning (or about to earn) from foreign clients, but there is a wall: how does money earned on Fiverr, Upwork or direct clients actually reach your Pakistani bank account? PayPal does not work in Pakistan, so freelancers use other legal channels. Choosing the right one affects your fees, exchange rate, and how fast money lands.

The marketplaces (Fiverr, Upwork)

  1. Payoneer is the standard bridge: both platforms pay out to Payoneer, which converts to PKR and sends to your local bank — usually 1-3 days, with the exchange rate shown before you withdraw. Set up Payoneer first (free account, KYC documents), link your Pakistani bank, then add it as your payout method on the platform.
  2. Direct bank transfer (where offered): Upwork offers "Direct to Local Bank" in PKR — one step instead of two, competitive rates. Fiverr's options evolve; check the platform's payout page before choosing.
  3. Keep it INSIDE the platform for marketplace clients: direct PayPal/bank deals with marketplace clients violate the platforms' rules and end accounts. Marketplace money exits only through marketplace payouts.

Direct clients (outside marketplaces)

  1. Bank direct transfer (SWIFT): works for larger invoices — give the client your IBAN; incoming remittances arrive through official channels. Fees per transfer are meaningful, so it suits $500+ invoices, not $50 ones.
  2. Wise: clients in supported countries send to your Wise details, and Wise forwards to your PKR bank — often the best rate and lowest combined fees for small-to-medium invoices. The client-side experience is just a local transfer for them.
  3. Payoneer's receiving accounts also bill direct clients directly (Payment Request) — handy if the client already knows Payoneer.

The practical rules that protect you

  • Fees and rates differ per channel: compare what ARRIVES in PKR for the same invoice, not the advertised fee.
  • Money arriving through proper banking channels builds your record — useful for proof-of-income letters, credit files, and future applications.
  • For tax handling on freelance income, a filing consultant costs little and prevents expensive surprises — this article covers receiving money, not tax law.
