My games are running at low FPS, how do I fix it

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Sep 1, 2026 · 3 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Sep 1, 2026

Your games should run smoothly at 60 FPS but crawl at 20–30, even on lower settings. Low FPS means the system cannot draw frames fast enough — and the bottleneck hides in one of five places: power settings, drivers, in-game settings, thermal throttling, or simply hardware below the game's demands. The fixes cost nothing and often double the FPS.

The five places, checked in order

  1. Power settings: a laptop on battery or "power saver" runs at half speed. Plug it in, and set Windows power mode to Best Performance (Settings → System → Power). Free FPS for one click.
  2. Drivers: update the graphics driver from NVIDIA/AMD/Intel's own site. Old drivers leaving 20-30% performance on the table is routine; new-game drivers arrive for every major release.
  3. In-game settings — the cheap ones first: shadows, ray tracing, volumetric fog, and crowd detail are the expensive ones; texture quality barely touches FPS on cards with enough memory. Cap FPS at your monitor's refresh rate, and test one setting at a time with a visible FPS counter.
  4. Thermal throttling: if FPS drops after ten minutes and gets worse the longer you play, heat is the culprit. Install HWMonitor/MSI Afterburner and watch temperatures — CPU/GPU in the 90s means cleaning dust, fixing fans, or (laptops) a cooling pad and hard surfaces.
  5. Background load: browser tabs with video, launchers updating in the background, RGB software — close them and watch the FPS counter. Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc) sorted by CPU/GPU shows who is stealing.

The honest diagnosis

If, after all five, the game still runs low: the hardware is below the game's demands — check the game's minimum requirements against your CPU/GPU. No setting rescues a machine below minimum; lowering resolution (the biggest lever of all) buys playability, and the settings-drop ladder in our FPS guide details which cuts cost the least visually.
