I forgot my PayPal password and cannot log in

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 8, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 8, 2026

You need PayPal for a payment, but the password escapes you — and PayPal locks accounts more strictly than most services after a few failed attempts, so guessing wildly makes things worse. The recovery flow uses your registered email or phone, with identity checks as backup. Getting back in is quick; the real lesson is turning on 2-step verification afterwards.

The recovery flow

  1. On the login page, click "Forgot password?" — do not keep guessing; failed attempts can trigger a longer lock.
  2. PayPal sends a reset code to your registered email or phone. Choose whichever you still control.
  3. Lost access to both? Use the "Having trouble logging in?" option on the help pages: PayPal verifies your identity another way — card details on the account, or identity documents. Have the card you linked ready; it is the fastest proof the account is yours.
  4. Still stuck? Contact PayPal support through the Help Center (message or call) — logged-out contact options exist for exactly this situation.
  5. Once back in: turn on 2-step verification immediately (Settings → Security). This is the step that makes the next recovery boring instead of terrifying — and check that your recovery email and phone on file are current.

A habit worth forming

Password managers end this entire category of problem — one strong master password, every account password unique and remembered for you.

Quick recap

  • Use the 'Forgot password' flow with access to your linked email or phone - that is the standard road back.
  • Still stuck after multiple tries? PayPal support can verify you with ID documents - use the official contact page only.
  • Watch for fake 'PayPal password reset' emails during this - real resets never ask for your card number.
  • Once in, add 2FA and confirm your recovery options are current.
