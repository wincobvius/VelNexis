My phone is stuck on the startup logo

VAbdul Rafay
CS student & tech writer · Aug 8, 2026 · 2 min read ✅ Editor-reviewed📅 Published Aug 8, 2026

You turn the phone on, the brand name appears — and stays there. Forever. This is a "boot loop": the phone starts, hits an error, restarts, and repeats. It usually follows a failed or interrupted software update, a bad app, or storage that filled up completely. The phone is usually not dead — but which fix works depends on how far gone it is.

Before you start: The later options here include a factory reset, which erases everything on the phone — photos, apps, chats, files. Try the earlier steps first. If you must reset and your data is not backed up, a repair shop can often rescue files first; ask before resetting.

Work up in this order

  1. Charge it for 30 minutes first. A deeply drained battery mimics a boot loop exactly. Many "dead" phones are just empty.
  2. Force a restart: hold Power + Volume Down for 15–30 seconds. The exact combination varies by brand — some use Power alone for 10+ seconds, some Power + Volume Up. A web search for "force restart" plus your model name gives the right one.
  3. Recovery mode: hold Power + Volume Up while booting. Choose Wipe cache partition if offered — it clears leftover update junk without touching your data.
  4. Factory reset from recovery mode — the erases-everything step, as described in the warning above.
  5. Still looping after a reset? The problem is hardware (often storage), and that means service center.

Quick recap

  • Charge it for 30 minutes first - a dead battery copies a dead phone.
  • Force restart: hold power 15-20 seconds (iPhone: volume up, down, then power).
  • Android recovery mode (power + volume) offers Wipe cache before any reset.
  • Recovery failing = software repair at a shop is cheaper than a new phone.
