Payments & Banking

Failed payments, deducted money, refunds and banking app issues — what to do and when.

Payment problems mix technology with trust. The technology side: a PayPal login that won't recover, a withdrawal stuck in a trading app, limits that make no sense — these have procedures, and the guides walk them step by step, from KYC verification to dispute windows. The trust side is the scam patterns that repeat every year: the fee that unlocks your money, the too-good exchange rate, the OTP someone asks you to read out, the card typed into a lookalike site. The guides tell you exactly when to freeze a card, how to report a fraud line, and which fights are worth your time. The rule underneath every article: no real service ever asks you to pay to receive your own money.
