Cloud Storage

Google Drive, OneDrive and iCloud issues — syncing, storage full, and sharing issues.

Cloud storage problems are quiet: Drive is full, Photos stopped backing up months ago, sync stuck halfway — and everything on your phone quietly at risk. This section covers the practical side: clearing 15GB sensibly, fixing stuck backups, moving files between Google accounts, and what to do before a school or college account gets deleted along with its files. The guides favor free tiers and built-in tools, and they flag the one mistake that costs people their photos every year: trusting a single copy of anything, anywhere.
