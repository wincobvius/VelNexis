Printer & Office

Printer, scanner and office document issues — offline errors, paper jams, PDF and Word issues.

Printers remain the most hated device in any home, mostly because their errors say nothing and mean everything. This section decodes the classics: printer shows offline, prints blank pages, refuses a new cartridge, smears ink, jams repeatedly. There are practical guides too — scanning without the bloated brand software, printing on both sides, choosing the right ink, and sharing one printer across every PC in the house. Each fix starts with the free, built-in tools before suggesting anything you pay for.

Printer patience, in one paragraph: run the built-in cleaning cycle before touching anything, match the cartridge code to the model sticker exactly, and give a WiFi printer a fixed IP address the day it arrives. Most guides here exist because one of those three was skipped. When a guide says the drum is worn, believe it — ghosting and repeated smears on laser printers are almost never anything else.
