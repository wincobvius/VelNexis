Camera & Photos

Phone and camera issues — camera not opening, blurry shots, and photo recovery.

Camera problems on phones are usually cheaper to fix than they look. This section covers the ones that come up most: blurry photos that are really dirty lenses, dark photos that are really exposure settings, black spots in every frame, cameras that refuse to open after a drop, and night mode that never seems to trigger. The guides show how to tell software faults from hardware faults with two-minute tests, so you know whether a restart fixes it or a repair shop should. When the fault is hardware, the repair options come with realistic costs.
