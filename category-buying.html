Buying Guides

What to buy and what to skip — honest checklists for phones, laptops and gadgets that respect your budget.

Buying tech in Pakistan is a skill. Prices are high, returns are rare, and every market holds both genuine bargains and polished traps. This section is built around checklists, not brand loyalty. The budget phone checklist walks through what actually matters at low prices — chipset, storage, update policy, battery — and what marketing wants you to look at instead. The laptop guide matches specs to fields of study, so a CS student and a business student don't buy the same machine. Storage decisions like SSD versus hard drive come with real numbers, not vague claims. There is a guide on when upgrading actually makes sense and one on why the cheapest option often costs the most by year two. Every recommendation shows its reasoning, prices are discussed as ranges rather than exact figures, and no guide contains an affiliate link — verdicts here are not for sale. When a buying decision is genuinely close — two phones at the same price, two laptops with the same specs — the guides say so plainly instead of inventing a winner, because the honest answer is often: it depends on how you use it, and here is how to tell which one fits you.
